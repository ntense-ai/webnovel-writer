# World Setting

## Power System
Realm order: Dou Qi → Dou Zhe → Dou Shi → Da Dou Shi → Dou Ling → Dou Wang → Dou Huang → Dou Zong → Dou Zun → Dou Sheng → Dou Di

Each major realm has one through nine stars. Breaking through to a new realm requires an opportunity and sufficient accumulated Dou Qi.

## Jia Nan Academy
One of the continent's leading academies. Admission includes a Dou Qi test and practical combat. Examiners may adjust the difficulty.

## Yao Lao / Yao Chen
Formerly a Dou Sheng expert, his severely injured soul inhabits an ancient ring. He can currently communicate with Xiao Yan only as a spirit and cannot directly affect the outside world.
