# World Setting

## Power System
Realm order: 斗之气 → 斗者 → 斗师 → 大斗师 → 斗灵 → 斗王 → 斗皇 → 斗宗 → 斗尊 → 斗圣 → 斗帝

Each major realm has one through nine stars. Breaking through to a new realm requires an opportunity and sufficient accumulated Dou Qi.

## Jia Nan Academy (迦南学院)
One of the continent's leading academies. Admission includes a Dou Qi test and practical combat. Examiners may adjust the difficulty.

## Yao Lao (药老 / 药尘)
Formerly a Dou Sheng expert, his severely injured soul inhabits an ancient ring. He can currently communicate with Xiao Yan (萧炎) only as a spirit and cannot directly affect the outside world.
