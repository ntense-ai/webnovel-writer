# Battle Through the Heavens (Test Master Outline)

## Basic Information
- Genre: Xuanhuan / cultivation
- Protagonist: Xiao Yan
- Core conflict: The three-year agreement

## Volume Directory

### Volume 1: The Xiao Family's Failure (Chapters 1-10)
Xiao Yan falls from genius to failure, unexpectedly obtains Yao Lao's ring, and resumes cultivation with the three-year showdown as his goal.

## Volume 1 Chapter Outlines

### Chapter 4: The Trial at Jia Nan Academy
- Goal: Xiao Yan takes Jia Nan Academy's admission test.
- Obstacles: The examiner looks down on his reputation as a failure.
- Cost: He must reveal some strength and expose his recovered cultivation.
- Core conflict: Hide his strength to protect the secret, or give everything to prove himself.
- Chapter change: He passes but attracts an elder's attention.
- Hook: The elder seems to recognize the aura of Yao Lao's ring.
