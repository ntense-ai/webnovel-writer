## Volume 1 Detailed Outline

### Chapter 4: The Trial at Jia Nan Academy

**Goal**: Xiao Yan takes Jia Nan Academy's admission test to prove he is not a failure.

**Obstacles**: Examiner Han Yue scorns his reputation and deliberately raises the difficulty.

**Cost**: Revealing some strength exposes his recovered cultivation and may attract factions coveting Yao Lao's ring.

**Core conflict**: Hide his strength to protect the secret vs give everything to prove himself.

**Antagonist tier**: Han Yue (minor obstacle).

**Chapter change**: Xiao Yan passes, but an elder notices his unusual aura.

**Unresolved question**: Has the elder recognized Yao Lao's ring? What consequences will follow?

**Hook**: After Xiao Yan leaves, the elder mutters, "That aura... impossible..."
