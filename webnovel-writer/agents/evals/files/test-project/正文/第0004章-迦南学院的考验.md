Morning light slowly seeped through the window crack, and Xiao Yan slowly opened his eyes. Determination flashed in his gaze, then gave way to calm.

He was very nervous. Today was Jia Nan Academy's admission test, and it would decide whether he could obtain enough resources before the three-year agreement came due. He told himself that he must not fail.

Xiao Yan slowly stood and stretched. Yao Lao's voice came from the ring: "Boy, are you ready?"

"Ready," Xiao Yan said mildly.

He left the room and ran into a youth in fine clothes. The youth looked him up and down, the corners of his lips rising slightly in disdain.

"Another piece of trash wants to enter Jia Nan Academy?" the richly dressed youth said coldly.

Xiao Yan did not answer, only walked quietly past. He knew arguing with someone like that was pointless. More importantly, he needed to conserve his strength for the coming test.

At the training ground, Xiao Yan saw the examiner, Han Yue. A Dou Shi expert renowned for strictness, Han Yue stood on a raised platform and surveyed the candidates with hawklike eyes.

First, Xiao Yan had to pass the Dou Qi test. Second, he had to demonstrate sufficient strength in combat. Finally, he needed the approval of at least one elder.

The test began. Xiao Yan pressed his palm against the testing stone, and Dou Qi slowly flowed from his body. The stone glowed faintly: nine-star Dou Zhe.

Han Yue's brow tightened slightly, surprise flashing in his eyes. Had this former failure really recovered to nine-star Dou Zhe?

"Not bad," Han Yue said mildly. "But nine-star Dou Zhe is nowhere near enough to enter Jia Nan Academy."

Xiao Yan's heart sank, but he quickly steadied himself. Yao Lao whispered from the ring: "Use suction to control your output. Don't reveal your full strength."

The combat test began. Xiao Yan faced an eight-star Dou Zhe candidate. His opponent struck first, punching at his chest. Xiao Yan sidestepped and reached out with his right hand, precisely catching the opponent's wrist.

The move displayed combat experience far beyond his level and caught the attention of a white-haired elder on the platform.

The elder's pupils contracted slightly. He seemed to sense something. That faint aura... impossible...

Xiao Yan ultimately passed the test. He felt relieved but also worried, with a trace of unease deep inside. He knew his secret might no longer be so safe. Nevertheless, he had taken an important step.

In conclusion, although today's test was full of challenges, Xiao Yan passed through his strength and wisdom. He knew the road ahead was long and the real trials were only beginning.
