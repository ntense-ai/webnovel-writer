## 1. Core Subgenres

### Cyberpunk
- **Features**: High tech, low life; cybernetic modification and megacorporate rule.
- **Core payoffs**: Hacking, implant upgrades, resisting the system.
- **Representative works**: *Cyber Heroes*, *2084*, *Mechanical Ascension*.

### Space Opera
- **Features**: Interstellar travel, alien civilizations, cosmic warfare.
- **Core payoffs**: Starship battles, alien exploration, clashes between civilizations.
- **Representative works**: *The Three-Body Problem*, *Foundation*, *Interstellar All-Round Evolution*.

### Hard Science Fiction
- **Features**: Rigorous scientific principles and realistic technical details.
- **Core payoffs**: Scientific mysteries, technological breakthroughs, logical deduction.
- **Representative works**: *The Three-Body Problem*, *The Martian*, *Ball Lightning*.

### Apocalyptic Science Fiction
- **Features**: Technological disasters, humanity's survival, rebuilding civilization.
- **Core payoffs**: Desperate survival, technology saving the world, moral choices.
- **Representative works**: *The Wandering Earth*, *Doomsday Wonderland*.

### Mecha/War
- **Features**: Giant mecha, interstellar warfare, passionate battles.
- **Core payoffs**: Piloting mecha, tactical command, ace pilots.
- **Representative works**: *Legend of the Supreme Soldier*, *Tempest of the Battlefield*, *The Legendary Mechanic*.

## 3. Technology System Design

### Core Technology Tree
| Field | Basic | Intermediate | Advanced |
|------|------|------|------|
| Energy | Fusion | Antimatter | Vacuum energy |
| Propulsion | Ion engines | Warp drive | Wormhole jumps |
| Weapons | Lasers/electromagnetics | Particle beams | Stellar cannons |
| Defense | Energy shields | Force fields | Spatial folding |
| Communication | Quantum communication | Faster-than-light | Consciousness links |
| Biology | Genetic modification | Cybernetics | Mind uploading |

### Cybernetic Modification Grades
- **Civilian**: Restore disabilities and enhance senses.
- **Military**: Combat enhancement and integrated weapons.
- **Experimental**: Exceed human limits, with side effects.
- **Forbidden**: Complete mechanization and loss of humanity.

### Technological Costs (Important)
- **Energy**: Advanced technology demands enormous power.
- **Side effects**: Implant rejection and genetic collapse.
- **Ethics**: Moral dilemmas surrounding cloning and mind uploading.
- **Loss of control**: AI rebellion and runaway weapons.

## 4. Classic Payoff Patterns

### Technological Dominance
- **Scene**: The protagonist possesses advanced technology.
- **Payoff**: High technology overwhelms low-tech enemies.
- **Key**: Give the technology a plausible origin (transmigration/inheritance/genius).

### Mecha Awakening
- **Scene**: The protagonist forms a special bond with a mecha.
- **Payoff**: Human and machine unite; combat power soars.
- **Key**: Make the mecha distinctive (AI/biological/ancient relic).

### Starship Command
- **Scene**: The protagonist becomes a captain and commands a battle.
- **Payoff**: Superior tactics defeat greater numbers.
- **Key**: Demonstrate command skill rather than effortless domination.

### Civilizational Contact
- **Scene**: First contact with an alien civilization.
- **Payoff**: Culture shock, technological exchange, resolving crises.
- **Key**: Give the alien civilization unique characteristics.

## 5. Outline Structure (Standard: 2 Million Chinese Characters)

### Volume One: Awakening (Chapters 1-80)
- **Core**: Gain a special ability/technology.
- **Setting**: One planet/space station.
- **Climax**: First demonstration of strength attracts attention.

### Volume Two: Rise (Chapters 81-180)
- **Core**: Join an organization and establish status.
- **Setting**: A stellar system.
- **Climax**: Complete a major mission and gain recognition.

### Volume Three: Supremacy (Chapters 181-350)
- **Core**: Become entangled in factional struggles.
- **Setting**: Multiple stellar systems.
- **Climax**: Become central to a faction.

### Volume Four: War (Chapters 351-550)
- **Core**: Participate in interstellar warfare.
- **Setting**: Galactic.
- **Climax**: Turn the tide and expose a conspiracy.

### Volume Five: Endgame (Chapters 551-700)
- **Core**: Face the ultimate threat.
- **Setting**: Cosmic.
- **Climax**: Save civilization/change the cosmic balance.

## 7. Alien Civilization Design

### Civilization Types
| Type | Features | Relationship with humans |
|------|------|-----------|
| Humanoid | Similar appearance | Easier communication |
| Silicon-based life | Fundamentally different | Hard to understand |
| Collective consciousness | Hive mind | Major conflict |
| Energy beings | Nonmaterial | Mysterious |
| Machine civilization | Evolved AI | Potential enemies or allies |

### First-Contact Protocol
- **Observation**: Observe without interference.
- **Probing**: Limited contact; assess threats.
- **Formal contact**: Establish diplomatic relations.
- **Conflict resolution**: War or peace.

## 8. Common Pitfalls

### Must Avoid
- ❌ Contradictory technological rules.
- ❌ Incomprehensible jargon overload.
- ❌ Omnipotent technology without costs.
- ❌ Aliens too similar to humans.

### Recommended Practices
- ✅ Keep technology internally consistent.
- ✅ Explain terminology gradually.
- ✅ Give technology clear limits.
- ✅ Make alien civilizations distinctive.
