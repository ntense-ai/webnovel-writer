# Republican-Era Romance Template

> Runtime preset identifier: `republican-era-romance` (English taxonomy and initialization preset).

> **Core appeal**: Turbulent times + secret identities + tangled love and hatred. Tell an unforgettable love story amid the storms of an unsettled era.

- Recommended constraint pack: Pack F10
- General overlay: Pack U01

## 1. Core Subgenres

### Elite-Family Feuds
- **Features**: Love and hatred within prominent Republican-era families.
- **Core payoffs**: Family struggles, identity reversals, anguished devotion.
- **Representative works**: *The Story of a Noble Family*, *Moment in Peking*, *Half a Lifelong Romance*, *Love in a Fallen City*.

### Love in Troubled Times
- **Features**: Romance against a wartime background.
- **Core payoffs**: Staying together amid chaos, loyalty unto death, love of home and country.
- **Representative works**: *The Road We Have Taken*, *All Quiet in Peking*, *The Disguiser*.

### Society Woman's Legend
- **Features**: The extraordinary life of a Republican-era society woman.
- **Core payoffs**: Female independence, the era's glamour, romantic choices.
- **Representative works**: *The Bund*, *Romance in the Rain*, *Love Story in Shanghai*.

### Espionage Romance
- **Features**: Love within an espionage setting.
- **Core payoffs**: Secret identities, tests of trust, shared life-and-death stakes.
- **Representative works**: *Sparrow*, *Rookie Agent Rouge*, *Qipao*, *The Disguiser*.

## 4. Classic Payoff Patterns

### Identity Reversal
- **Scene**: Someone of humble status is actually a well-born lady/revolutionary.
- **Payoff**: Truth silences those who dismissed them.
- **Key**: Foreshadow the revelation.

### Together Through Turmoil
- **Scene**: The lovers refuse to abandon each other in war.
- **Payoff**: Profoundly moving devotion through life and death.
- **Key**: Make the danger real.

### Family Acceptance
- **Scene**: Overcome obstacles to win family approval.
- **Payoff**: Lovers finally unite.
- **Key**: Show the heroine's admirable qualities.

### Successful Revenge
- **Scene**: Take revenge against those who caused harm.
- **Payoff**: Justice is served and readers feel vindicated.
- **Key**: Revenge needs moral boundaries.

## 5. Outline Structure (Standard: 1 Million Chinese Characters)

### Volume One: Meeting (Chapters 1-60)
- **Core**: Destinies intertwine and feelings emerge.
- **Relationship**: Strangers → acquaintances.
- **Climax**: First romantic spark.

### Volume Two: Understanding (Chapters 61-150)
- **Core**: Deeper understanding and growing affection.
- **Relationship**: Acquaintances → romantic ambiguity.
- **Climax**: Recognize their feelings.

### Volume Three: Trials (Chapters 151-250)
- **Core**: Family/historical obstacles.
- **Relationship**: Romantic ambiguity → crisis.
- **Climax**: Forced separation.

### Volume Four: Protection (Chapters 251-350)
- **Core**: Confront everything for love.
- **Relationship**: Crisis → reunion.
- **Climax**: Overcome the obstacles.

### Volume Five: Fulfillment (Chapters 351-400)
- **Core**: The lovers finally unite.
- **Relationship**: Reunion → lifelong commitment.
- **Climax**: Satisfying resolution.

## 7. Creating Period Atmosphere

- Use period forms of address: madam, miss, sir.
- Include classical vocabulary appropriately.
- Avoid modern internet slang.

## 8. Common Pitfalls

### Must Avoid
- ❌ Weak period atmosphere.
- ❌ Conflicts reduced to melodramatic contrivance.
- ❌ An excessively naive heroine.
- ❌ Basic historical errors.

### Recommended Practices
- ✅ Accurate period details.
- ✅ Conflicts with depth.
- ✅ A heroine who grows.
- ✅ Plausible historical background.
