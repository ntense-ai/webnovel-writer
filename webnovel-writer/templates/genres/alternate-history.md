# High-Concept Historical Fiction Template

> Runtime preset identifier: `alternate-history` (English taxonomy and initialization preset).

> **Core appeal**: Historical framework + premise reversals + rule-based contests. When supernatural rules intertwine with history, what does changing history cost?

---

## Recommended Creative Constraints (Optional)
- Recommended constraint pack: Pack M08
- General overlay: Pack U02

## 1. Core Subgenres

### History as Explicit Rules
- **Features**: Historical patterns become tangible, usable rules.
- **Core payoffs**: Exploiting rules, rewriting history, contests of causality.
- **Representative works**: *Joy of Life*, *My Heroic Husband*, *Dawn in Lingao*.

### Hidden Historical Wonders
- **Features**: Supernatural truths concealed behind history.
- **Core payoffs**: Revelations, unraveling conspiracies, reconstructing history.
- **Representative works**: *The Longest Day in Chang'an*, *Tang Dynasty Mysteries*, *The Ming Dynasty Under a Microscope*.

### Time Travel to Change Fate
- **Features**: A transmigrator tries to alter history's course.
- **Core payoffs**: Butterfly effects, historical turning points, resisting fate.
- **Representative works**: *A Step into the Past*, *Return to the Ming Dynasty as a Prince*, *New Song*.

### Historical Game
- **Features**: History becomes gamified/systematized.
- **Core payoffs**: Using systems, completing quests, intervening in history.
- **Representative works**: *My Leisurely Life in Great Kang*, *The Ming National Preceptor*.

## 3. Rule System Design

### Historical Intervention Rules
```
Changeable:
- Individual destinies.
- Local events.
- Technological development.

Difficult to change:
- Broad historical trends.
- Pivotal moments.
- Destinies of central figures.

Unchangeable:
- Time-locked events.
- Events protected by causality.
```

### Cost System
| Cost type | Features | Example |
|----------|------|------|
| Equivalent exchange | Gain something, lose something | Saving someone requires sacrifice |
| Causal backlash | Greater changes mean greater backlash | Physical/mental damage |
| Temporal debt | Borrow from the future and repay | Shortened lifespan |
| Diluted existence | Diminished presence | Forgotten by history |

### Using Historical Knowledge
| Knowledge | Use | Risk |
|----------|----------|------|
| Historical events | Prepare in advance | Butterfly effects |
| Technical knowledge | Technological superiority | Incompatible with the era |
| Information on people | Recruit talent | Alter their fate |
| Economic patterns | Accumulate wealth | Attract attention |

## 4. Classic Payoff Patterns

### Historical Foresight
- **Scene**: Predict events using historical knowledge.
- **Payoff**: Foresight makes preparations succeed.
- **Key**: Provide a plausible information source.

### Exploiting Rules
- **Scene**: Discover and use historical rules.
- **Payoff**: Turn rules into an advantage.
- **Key**: Rules must be consistent.

### Historical Reversal
- **Scene**: Change history's course.
- **Payoff**: Butterfly effects reconstruct history.
- **Key**: Changes require a cost.

### Causal Contest
- **Scene**: Contend with historical rules/guardians.
- **Payoff**: Intellectual competition and risky victories.
- **Key**: Both sides must be intelligent.

## 7. Balancing History and High Concepts

### Historical Foundations
```
Must be accurate:
- Major historical events.
- Key historical figures.
- Basic period background.

May be invented:
- Supplemental details.
- Peripheral characters.
- Secret events.

High-concept freedom:
- Rule systems.
- Supernatural elements.
- Parallel histories.
```

### Maintaining Plausibility
| Area | Key point | Pitfall |
|------|------|------|
| Technology | Gradual progression | Excessive leaps |
| Institutions | Fit the era | Too far ahead |
| Ideas | Established groundwork | Modern thinking |
| Language | Period flavor | Internet slang |

### Integrating Concept and History
- Rules must fit the historical background.
- Supernatural elements need historical explanations.
- Changing history requires a reasonable price.

---

## 8. Common Pitfalls

### Must Avoid
- ❌ An excessively fantasy-like premise.
- ❌ Confused causal logic.
- ❌ Errors in basic historical knowledge.
- ❌ Costs that never materialize.

### Recommended Practices
- ✅ Integrate the premise with history.
- ✅ Clear causality.
- ✅ Accurate historical foundations.
- ✅ Explicit costs that are paid.
