# Career, Marriage, and Romance Template

> Runtime preset identifier: `workplace-romance` (English taxonomy and initialization preset).

> **Core appeal**: Workplace contests + growth and promotion + emotional balance. Find a personal equilibrium between career and love.

---

## Recommended Creative Constraints (Optional)
- Recommended constraint pack: Pack F09
- General overlay: Pack U02

## 1. Core Subgenres

### Office Romance
- **Features**: Relationships between managers/subordinates or colleagues.
- **Core payoffs**: Secret crushes fulfilled, identity reversals, affectionate daily life.
- **Representative works**: *Boss & Me*, *My Sunshine*, *You Are My Glory*.

### Career Comeback
- **Features**: A novice grows into a professional elite.
- **Core payoffs**: Proving ability, promotion, success in career and love.
- **Representative works**: *Ode to Joy*, *The First Half of My Life*, *All Is Well*.

### Business Competition
- **Features**: Love and hatred amid commercial rivalry.
- **Core payoffs**: Business victories, rivals conceding, powerful partnerships.
- **Representative works**: *Entrepreneurial Age*, *Game of Hunting*, *The Best Partner*.

### Industry Elite
- **Features**: Professional stories within a specific industry.
- **Core payoffs**: Expertise, industry recognition, becoming a professional legend.
- **Representative works**: *Surgeons*, *Medical Examiner Dr. Qin*, *The Interpreter*.

## 3. Career System Design

### Advancement Paths
```
Specialist path:
Intern → Junior → Intermediate → Senior → Expert → Chief specialist

Management path:
Employee → Team lead → Supervisor → Manager → Director → VP → CEO

Entrepreneurial path:
Employee → Partner → Founder → Listed-company owner
```

### Workplace Contests
| Type | Method | Risk |
|------|------|------|
| Performance competition | Compete on ability | Low |
| Resource competition | Secure projects | Medium |
| Alignment choices | Choose a patron | High |
| Office politics | Build factions | Very high |

### Workplace Rules
```
Explicit rules:
- Performance reviews.
- Promotion systems.
- Rewards and penalties.

Unwritten rules:
- Factional alignment.
- Social obligations and tact.
- Information asymmetry.
```

## 4. Classic Payoff Patterns

### Proving Ability
- **Scene**: Demonstrate competence after being doubted.
- **Payoff**: Expertise leaves opponents speechless.
- **Key**: Support it with professional detail.

### Promotion Comeback
- **Scene**: Rise from the bottom to a senior position.
- **Payoff**: Former detractors must now look up to the protagonist.
- **Key**: Show the growth process.

### Business Victory
- **Scene**: Win a crucial project/negotiation.
- **Payoff**: Defeat rivals and gain recognition.
- **Example**: A creative proposal wins a major client.

### Identity Reversal
- **Scene**: A hidden identity is revealed.
- **Payoff**: The person is actually a major owner/wealthy heir.
- **Key**: Foreshadow it; avoid abruptness.

## 7. Industry-Writing Techniques

### Creating Professional Credibility
- Use industry terminology, but explain it.
- Make workflows plausible.
- Keep workplace rules realistic.

### Common Industry Settings
| Industry | Core scenes | Professional elements |
|------|----------|----------|
| Finance | Trading/roadshows | Valuation/due diligence |
| Law | Court/negotiations | Cases/clauses |
| Medicine | Surgery/consultation | Case histories/procedures |
| Internet | Products/fundraising | Users/data |
| Media | Interviews/publication | Story selection/traffic |

### Workplace Atmosphere
```
Everyday scenes:
- Meeting-room maneuvering.
- Break-room gossip.
- Overtime to finish a project.
- Team-building activities.

Key scenes:
- Performance-review presentations.
- Project reports.
- Business negotiations.
- Crisis public relations.
```

## 8. Common Pitfalls

### Must Avoid
- ❌ Unrealistic workplace portrayals.
- ❌ Romance without actual work.
- ❌ Promotions too quick and effortless.
- ❌ Incorrect professional knowledge.

### Recommended Practices
- ✅ Authentic workplace details.
- ✅ Balance career and romance.
- ✅ Show the promotion process.
- ✅ Accurate professional knowledge.
