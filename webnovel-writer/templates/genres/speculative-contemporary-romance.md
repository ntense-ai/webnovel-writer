# High-Concept Contemporary Romance Template

> Runtime preset identifier: `speculative-contemporary-romance` (English taxonomy and initialization preset).

> **Core appeal**: Real-world relationships + rules/anomalies + emotional reversals. How can love and rules coexist when supernatural rules invade modern romance?

## 1. Core Subgenres

### Rule-Bound Romance
- **Features**: Romance is constrained by particular rules.
- **Core payoffs**: Exploiting and breaking rules; sincerity triumphs over them.
- **Representative works**: *Begin Again*, *Left Right*, *The First Half of My Life*.

### Contractual Romance
- **Features**: Love begins through a contract/transaction.
- **Core payoffs**: Pretend feelings become real, contract reversals, genuine affection emerges.
- **Representative works**: *My Sunshine*, *Boss & Me*, *Love O2O*.

### Rebirth and Revenge
- **Features**: Change fate after rebirth and find true love.
- **Core payoffs**: Avoid pitfalls, seize opportunities, take revenge, gain a loving partner.
- **Representative works**: *Revive*, *The Concubine's Daughter's Strategy*, *Rebirth of the Malicious Imperial Consort*.

### Superpowered Romance
- **Features**: Modern love involving special abilities.
- **Core payoffs**: Powers help romance; secret identities; powers versus love.
- **Representative works**: *Mind Reading*, *In Time*, *That Time I Got Reincarnated as a Slime*.

## 3. Rules and Emotion

### Rule Design Principles
```
Connected to romance:
- Affect the relationship's development.
- Generate emotional conflict.
- Test sincerity.

Exploitable loopholes:
- Genuine feelings can overcome them.
- Specific conditions can lift them.
- Costs are bearable.

Explicit costs:
- Violations incur punishment.
- Breakthroughs carry a price.
- Choices require trade-offs.
```

### Conflict Between Love and Rules
| Conflict | Manifestation | Resolution |
|----------|------|----------|
| Prohibition | Cannot be together | Break the rule |
| Compulsion | Must separate | Find a loophole |
| Trial | Forced choice | Prove sincerity |
| Cost | Being together carries a price | Willingly pay it |

### Cost System
| Cost | Features | Emotional meaning |
|----------|------|----------|
| Memory | Forget the partner | Anguish |
| Ability | Lose powers | Sacrifice |
| Identity | Change identity | Choice |
| Time | Lose time | Cherishing what remains |

## 4. Classic Payoff Patterns

### Breaking Rules
- **Scene**: Genuine affection breaks a restriction.
- **Payoff**: Love conquers all.
- **Key**: Provide sufficient emotional groundwork.

### Identity Revealed
- **Scene**: A hidden identity/ability comes to light.
- **Payoff**: A surprising reversal silences doubters.
- **Key**: Foreshadow it.

### Rewriting Fate
- **Scene**: Change an originally tragic destiny.
- **Payoff**: Avoid tragedy and find happiness.
- **Key**: Show the contrast.

### Proving Sincerity
- **Scene**: Pass a test of genuine love.
- **Payoff**: Moving emotion, sweetness, fulfillment.
- **Key**: The test must matter.

## 5. Outline Structure (Standard: 800,000 Chinese Characters)

### Volume One: Meeting (Chapters 1-50)

- **Relationship**: Strangers → acquaintances.
- **Climax**: Discover the rules/abilities.

### Volume Two: Understanding (Chapters 51-120)
- **Core**: Interact within the rules.
- **Relationship**: Acquaintances → romantic ambiguity.
- **Climax**: Recognize their feelings.

### Volume Three: Trials (Chapters 121-200)

## 7. Balancing Reality and High Concepts

### Real-World Foundation
```
Preserve realism:
- Modern urban setting.
- Authentic relationships.
- Plausible social rules.

High-concept elements:
- Supernatural rules.
- Special abilities.
- Arrangements of fate.
```

### Emotional Authenticity
| Area | Key point | Pitfall |
|------|------|------|
| Relationship development | Gradual | Too fast or abrupt |
| Character reactions | Consistent with personality | Serving plot alone |
| Conflict resolution | Plausible | Excessive coincidence |
| Ending | Established groundwork | Forced happy ending |

### Integrating Rules with Daily Life
- Embed rules in everyday living.
- Limit ability usage.
- Explain supernatural elements.

---

## 8. Common Pitfalls

### Must Avoid
- ❌ Excessively abstract rules.
- ❌ Unrealistic relationships.
- ❌ Arbitrary rule changes.
- ❌ Costs that never materialize.

### Recommended Practices
- ✅ Integrate rules with reality.
- ✅ Natural emotional development.
- ✅ Consistent rules.
- ✅ Explicit costs that are paid.
