# Sweet Youth Romance Template

> Runtime preset identifier: `young-sweet-romance` (English taxonomy and initialization preset).

> **Core appeal**: Light sweetness + frequent interaction + relatable growth. Meet the right person in the most beautiful years of life.

## 1. Core Subgenres

### Campus Romance
- **Features**: Innocent love in high school/university.
- **Core payoffs**: Romantic ambiguity, heart-stirring confessions, affectionate daily life.
- **Representative works**: *A Love So Beautiful*, *My Huckleberry Friends*, *With You*.

### Childhood Friends
- **Features**: Growing up together develops into love.
- **Core payoffs**: Natural chemistry, changing relationship roles, protective growth.
- **Representative works**: *My Sunshine*, *Love O2O*.

### Bickering Lovers
- **Features**: Initial dislike and banter become genuine love.
- **Core payoffs**: Everyday teasing, endearing contrasts, sincere feelings.
- **Representative works**: *Boss & Me*, *Go Go Squid!*.

### Healing Sweetness
- **Features**: Warmth, healing, and gradual affection.
- **Core payoffs**: Cozy routines, mutual healing, simple happiness.
- **Representative works**: *You Are My Glory*, *The Oath of Love*.

## 4. Classic Payoff Patterns

### Rescue
- **Scene**: The heroine faces trouble/danger.
- **Payoff**: The male lead arrives in time and firmly protects her.
- **Key**: Make it natural rather than contrived.

### Jealous Declaration
- **Scene**: Someone approaches the other lead.
- **Payoff**: Jealousy prompts a declaration of attachment.
- **Key**: Keep it endearing, not excessive.

### Public Affection
- **Scene**: Show affection before others.
- **Payoff**: Onlookers are envious.
- **Key**: Natural, not affected.

### Unexpected Shared Living
- **Scene**: Circumstances lead them to live together.
- **Payoff**: Everyday interactions intensify romantic tension.
- **Key**: Provide a plausible reason.

## 8. Common Pitfalls

### Must Avoid
- ❌ Unvarying sweetness.
- ❌ Fake conflicts and forced anguish.
- ❌ A flawless male lead.
- ❌ An excessively naive heroine.

### Recommended Practices
- ✅ Sweetness with rhythm and variation.
- ✅ Plausible, relatable conflicts.
- ✅ A flawed male lead who grows.
- ✅ A heroine with agency and growth.
