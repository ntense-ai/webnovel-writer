# Fantasy Romance Template

> Runtime preset identifier: `幻想言情` (preserved for taxonomy and initialization compatibility).

> **Core appeal**: Otherworldly rules + emotional sacrifice + overturned destiny. Tell a love story crossing species and fate in a fantastic world.

- Recommended constraint pack: Pack F11
- General overlay: Pack U01

## 1. Core Subgenres

### Immortal-Cultivation Romance
- **Features**: Love and hatred in a cultivation world.
- **Core payoffs**: Cultivating together, enduring tribulations together, love across lifetimes.
- **Representative works**: *Eternal Love*, *The Journey of Flower*, *Ashes of Love*, *Love and Redemption*.

### Eastern Fantasy Romance
- **Features**: Fantastic love on an invented continent.
- **Core payoffs**: Contractual bonds, interspecies taboos, destined partners.
- **Representative works**: The romantic threads of *Soul Land*, *Battle Through the Heavens*, *Martial Universe*.

### Western Fantasy Romance
- **Features**: Love in a Western fantasy setting.
- **Core payoffs**: Forbidden interspecies love, magical bonds, royal marriages.
- **Representative works**: *Twilight*, *The Vampire Diaries*, the romantic threads of *The Lord of the Rings*.

### Mythological Romance
- **Features**: Love among gods, immortals, spirits, and demons.
- **Core payoffs**: Divine couples, forbidden mortal-divine love, fidelity across reincarnations.
- **Representative works**: *Love and Destiny*, *Goodbye My Princess*, *Lost You Forever*, *The Legend of Shen Li*.

## 4. Classic Payoff Patterns

### Destined Meeting
- **Scene**: A fated encounter.
- **Payoff**: Past and present lives connect through destiny.
- **Key**: Establish the past life beforehand.

### Forbidden Love
- **Scene**: Romance crosses species/status boundaries.
- **Payoff**: Confront everything for love.
- **Key**: The obstacles must be real.

### Sacrificial Protection
- **Scene**: Sacrifice everything for the other person.
- **Payoff**: Moving emotion, anguish, and sweetness.
- **Key**: The sacrifice must matter.

### Renewal After Suffering
- **Scene**: Reunion after hardship.
- **Payoff**: Suffering gives way to a happy ending.
- **Key**: Establish sufficient anguish first.

## 5. Outline Structure (Standard: 1 Million Chinese Characters)

### Volume One: Meeting (Chapters 1-60)
- **Core**: A destined encounter and emerging affection.
- **Relationship**: Strangers → acquaintances.
- **Climax**: First romantic spark.

### Volume Two: Understanding (Chapters 61-150)
- **Core**: Deeper understanding and growing affection.
- **Relationship**: Acquaintances → romantic ambiguity.
- **Climax**: Recognize their feelings.

### Volume Three: Trials (Chapters 151-250)
- **Core**: Taboos/obstacles emerge.
- **Relationship**: Romantic ambiguity → separation.
- **Climax**: Forced parting.

### Volume Four: Protection (Chapters 251-350)
- **Core**: Confront everything for love.
- **Relationship**: Separation → reunion.
- **Climax**: Break the taboo.

### Volume Five: Fulfillment (Chapters 351-400)
- **Core**: Lovers finally unite.
- **Relationship**: Reunion → lifelong devotion.
- **Climax**: Satisfying resolution.

## 8. Common Pitfalls

### Must Avoid
- ❌ Unclear rules.
- ❌ Forced romantic progression.
- ❌ Meaningless suffering.
- ❌ Tool-like supporting characters.

### Recommended Practices
- ✅ Clear, consistent rules.
- ✅ Established emotional groundwork.
- ✅ Suffering with purpose and payoff.
- ✅ Fully realized supporting characters.
