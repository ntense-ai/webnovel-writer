# High-Concept Urban Fiction Template

> Runtime preset identifier: `urban-speculative` (English taxonomy and initialization preset).

> **Core appeal**: Realistic cities + invading rules/anomalies + major reversals. How can ordinary people survive when supernatural rules intrude into everyday life?

---

## Recommended Creative Constraints (Optional)
- Recommended constraint packs: Pack M04 / Pack M05
- General overlay: Pack U02

## 1. Core Subgenres

### Rule Invasion
- **Features**: Supernatural rules invade reality.
- **Core payoffs**: Discovering, exploiting, and turning rules against threats.
- **Representative works**: *I Learned to Slay Gods in a Mental Hospital*, *Return from the Abyss*, *Lord of Mysteries*.

### Urban Anomalies
- **Features**: Strange phenomena appear throughout the city.
- **Core payoffs**: Exploring anomalies, revealing truth, awakening powers.
- **Representative works**: *My House of Horrors*, *Strange Records of the Civil Investigation Bureau*, *Supernatural Livestream Room*.

### System Arrival
- **Features**: Systems/gamification invade reality.
- **Core payoffs**: Exploiting systems, completing quests, dominating through levels.
- **Representative works**: *Global Martial Arts*, *My Cell Prison*, *Global Catastrophe*.

### Parallel Worlds
- **Features**: Discover/travel to parallel worlds.
- **Core payoffs**: World exploration, differing rules, switching identities.
- **Representative works**: *Reincarnation Paradise*, *Terror Infinity*, *Global Evolution*.

## 3. Rule System Design

### Rule Types
| Type | Features | Example |
|------|------|------|
| Prohibition | Cannot do something | Cannot speak a name |
| Obligation | Must do something | Must respond |
| Conditional | Triggers under conditions | Seeing it causes... |
| Time-bound | Active at a certain time | Three in the morning |
| Spatial | Active in a certain place | Inside an elevator |

### Design Principles
```
Consistency:
- No contradictions within a rule.
- Logical relationships between rules.

Verifiability:
- Clear consequences for violation.
- Clear benefits for compliance.

Exploitability:
- Loopholes exist.
- Rules can be turned against threats.

Origins:
- Rules formed for a reason.
- Their roots can be traced.
```

### Cost System
| Cost type | Features | Recovery |
|----------|------|----------|
| Physical | Bodily damage | Time/treatment |
| Mental | Mental depletion | Rest/special methods |
| Resource | Consumed items | Acquisition/crafting |
| Existential | Reduced presence/existence | Special methods |

## 4. Classic Payoff Patterns

### Decoding Rules
- **Scene**: Discover a loophole.
- **Payoff**: Exploit it to escape/defeat a threat.
- **Key**: Make the solution plausible, not excessively coincidental.

### Ability Awakening
- **Scene**: A special ability awakens in a life-or-death crisis.
- **Payoff**: Defeat the enemy and reverse the situation.
- **Key**: The ability needs limits.

### Revealing Truth
- **Scene**: Uncover the truth behind an anomaly.
- **Payoff**: Realization connects earlier and later details.
- **Key**: Use foreshadowing and setup.

### Level Dominance
- **Scene**: Higher-level abilities overwhelm a low-level anomaly.
- **Payoff**: An easy resolution demonstrates growth.
- **Key**: Show the growth process first.

## 5. Outline Structure (Standard: 1.5 Million Chinese Characters)

### Volume One: Initiation (Chapters 1-60)
- **Core**: Encounter the anomalous world.
- **Abilities**: Initial awakening.
- **Climax**: Resolve the first anomalous incident.

### Volume Two: Growth (Chapters 61-150)
- **Core**: Improve abilities and understanding.
- **Abilities**: Steady improvement.
- **Climax**: Resolve a major anomalous incident.

### Volume Three: Going Deeper (Chapters 151-280)
- **Core**: Explore the anomalous world's truth.
- **Abilities**: Qualitative transformation.
- **Climax**: Reveal part of the truth.

### Volume Four: Confrontation (Chapters 281-400)
- **Core**: Fight high-level anomalies/factions.
- **Abilities**: Near the pinnacle.
- **Climax**: Defeat the main enemy.

### Volume Five: Endgame (Chapters 401-500)
- **Core**: Final truth and ultimate confrontation.
- **Abilities**: Pinnacle.
- **Climax**: Satisfying resolution.

## 7. Atmosphere

### Ordinary Life Versus Anomalies
```
Everyday scenes:
- Work/school.
- Shopping/entertainment.
- Socializing/rest.

Anomalous intrusion:
- Familiar scenes become unfamiliar.
- Everyday rules stop working.
- Safety collapses.
```

### Horror Atmosphere
| Type | Technique | Effect |
|------|------|------|
| Psychological | Suggestion/omission | Imagined fear |
| Rule-based | Violation means death | Tension and anxiety |
| Unknown | Insufficient information | Unease |
| Reversal | Overturned understanding | Shock |

### Pacing
```
Setup (20%):
- Anomalies in everyday life.
- Ominous signs.

Escalation (40%):
- Anomalous incidents occur.
- Danger intensifies.

Climax (30%):
- Confront the central anomaly.
- Life hangs by a thread.

Resolution (10%):
- Anomaly ends/continues.
- Lingering resonance/foreshadowing.
```

## 8. Common Pitfalls

### Must Avoid
- ❌ Self-contradictory rules.
- ❌ Costs that never materialize.
- ❌ Reversals so frequent they become unbelievable.
- ❌ Cost-free protagonist cheats.

### Recommended Practices
- ✅ Consistent, verifiable rules.
- ✅ Explicit costs that are actually paid.
- ✅ Foreshadowed reversals.
- ✅ A protagonist who pays costs and grows.
