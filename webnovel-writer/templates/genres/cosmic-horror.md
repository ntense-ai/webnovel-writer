# Lovecraftian Fiction Template

> Runtime preset identifier: `cosmic-horror` (English taxonomy and initialization preset).

> **Core appeal**: The indescribable + collapsing rationality + the price of truth. Reader satisfaction comes from approaching a truth that cannot be endured.

---

## Recommended Creative Constraints (Optional)
- Recommended constraint pack: Pack M21
- General overlay: Pack U04

## 1. Core Subgenres

### Investigative Archives
- **Features**: Cases/logs/manuscripts drive a puzzle-like narrative.
- **Core payoffs**: Connecting clues, revealing truth, evidence that overturns assumptions.
- **Best for**: Mystery readers and rational deduction.

### Rituals Out of Control
- **Features**: Forbidden rituals trigger disasters with escalating consequences.
- **Core payoffs**: Stopping escalation, exchanging costs, partial victories.
- **Best for**: Intense conflict and crisis.

### Invasion of the Old Ones
- **Features**: Ancient gods' rules contaminate reality's order.
- **Core payoffs**: Establishing new survival rules amid collapsing order.
- **Best for**: Long-form, worldbuilding-driven stories.

### On the Edge of Sanity
- **Features**: The protagonist maintains the ability to act while losing sanity.
- **Core payoffs**: Spending small remnants of reason for crucial victories.
- **Best for**: Strong psychological characterization.

## 2. Worldbuilding: Rules of Horror

### Layers of Truth
| Layer | Information | Cost |
|------|--------|------|
| Surface anomaly | Explainable deviations | Fear and misjudgment |
| Intermediate contamination | Distorted rules | Declining sanity, broken relationships |
| Deep truth | Cosmic hostility | Collapse of identity, irreversible losses |

### Faction Layers
- **Investigative organizations**: Try to contain anomalies.
- **Secret cults**: Seek an ancient god's arrival or benefits from sacrifice.
- **Neutral observers**: Possess intelligence but refuse responsibility for the consequences.
- **Contaminated individuals**: Affected people or locations.

---

## 3. Classic Payoff Patterns

### Deciphering Rules
- **Scene**: An incomprehensible locked room/ruin/ritual text.
- **Payoff**: The protagonist deciphers a key rule through small details.
- **Key**: Rules must be consistent and verifiable.

### Victory at a Price
- **Scene**: Emerging unscathed is impossible.
- **Payoff**: The protagonist pays a defined cost for an interim victory.
- **Key**: The cost must have real consequences in later chapters.

### False-Truth Reversal
- **Scene**: An apparently solved case is only an entrance to something deeper.
- **Payoff**: Earlier details pay off in a second reversal.
- **Key**: Reversals rely on planted clues, not sudden announcements.

### Holding the Sanity Line
- **Scene**: The protagonist is on the brink of collapse.
- **Payoff**: An obsession or promise holds their mind together long enough to finish the task.
- **Key**: Plant the emotional anchor beforehand.

## 4. Outline Structure (Reference: 1.8 Million Chinese Characters)

### Volume One: Strange Sounds (Chapters 1-70)
- **Core**: Discover anomalies and the first rule.
- **Climax**: Escape a contaminated zone alive for the first time.

### Volume Two: Deep Dive (Chapters 71-180)
- **Core**: Expand the investigation network; organizations confront cults.
- **Climax**: A key character's contamination becomes uncontrollable.

### Volume Three: Rifts (Chapters 181-320)
- **Core**: Local rules of the world are rewritten.
- **Climax**: A costly victory saves the central city.

### Volume Four: Reset (Chapters 321-500)
- **Core**: The protagonist confronts the deepest truth.
- **Climax**: Sacrifice and sealing reconstruct a new order.

---

## 5. Common Pitfalls

### Must Avoid
- ❌ Frightening descriptions without coherent rules.
- ❌ Omnipotent ancient gods that leave no possible resolution.
- ❌ Costs claimed in dialogue but never felt.
- ❌ Reversals forced into the final chapter without setup.

### Recommended Practices
- ✅ Give every anomaly at least one verifiable rule.
- ✅ Every advance costs the protagonist something specific.
- ✅ Sustain serial-reading satisfaction with partial victories.
- ✅ Ensure reversals have clues readers can trace backward.
