# Cultivation Fiction Template

> Runtime preset identifier: `修仙` (preserved for taxonomy and initialization compatibility).

> **Core appeal**: Defying fate + pursuing immortality + ruthless laws. Cultivation means competing with heaven for life and with other people for resources.

## 1. Core Subgenres

### Mortal
- **Features**: An ordinarily gifted protagonist rises through strategy, caution, and a special advantage.
- **Core payoffs**: Outsmarting opponents, accumulating small gains, and concealing strength to catch enemies off guard.
- **Representative work**: *A Record of a Mortal's Journey to Immortality*.

### Invincible
- **Features**: Starts at the pinnacle (or with an invincible background/master).
- **Core payoffs**: Crushing every obstacle, silencing challengers, and breaking the rules.
- **Representative work**: *Emperor's Domination*.

### Clan
- **Features**: The protagonist leads a clan's rise through development and management.
- **Core payoffs**: Collective ascension, compounding resources, and factional competition.
- **Key**: Unity (avoid clan infighting; present a united front).

### Cautious
- **Features**: Avoids karmic entanglements and dangerous situations; maximizes defense.
- **Core payoffs**: Surviving after every enemy has died. Outliving an opponent is also victory.
- **Reversal**: When forced to act, reveals overwhelming strength because of exhaustive preparation.

## 2. Worldbuilding: Cultivation Sociology

### Dark-Forest Rules
- **Limited resources**: Spiritual energy, medicinal herbs, and mineral veins are scarce.
- **Murder for treasure**: Upon meeting a strange cultivator in the wilderness, the first thought is "Will they kill me?" and the second is "Can I kill them?"
- **Possession invites danger**: Owning a treasure without the strength to protect it is a fatal liability.

### Class Barriers
- **Independent cultivators**: The lowest social tier, risking their lives for a handful of spirit stones.
- **Sect disciples**: Organized and backed by powerful patrons; they look down on independent cultivators.
- **Great-clan descendants**: Monopolize resources and inherited bloodlines.

---

## 3. Four-Step Opportunity Arc

Do not simply let the protagonist stumble upon treasure. Use:

1. **Rumor**: Tavern gossip, auction clues, or ancient records.
2. **Exploration**: Enter a secret realm/ruin and encounter traps, formations, and beasts.
3. **Conflict**: Treasure appears; multiple factions fight. The protagonist exploits the chaos or overwhelms the opposition.
4. **Reward**: Obtain the treasure and immediately convert it into power (e.g., an on-the-spot breakthrough).

---

## 4. Realms and Combat Power

### Standard Realm Progression
1. **Qi Refining**: Draw spiritual energy into the body; basic spells emerge. (Lifespan: 100)
2. **Foundation Establishment**: Build the foundation of cultivation; fly on a sword. (Lifespan: 200)
3. **Golden Core**: Form a golden core and take destiny into one's own hands. (Lifespan: 500)
4. **Nascent Soul**: Break the core to form a nascent soul; possess another body if the physical body is destroyed. (Lifespan: 1,000)
5. **Spirit Transformation**: Comprehend laws and give form to spiritual consciousness. (Lifespan: 2,000)
6. **Void Refinement** → **Body Integration** → **Mahayana** → **Tribulation Transcendence**.

### Preventing Power-Scaling Collapse
- **Major-realm suppression**: A Foundation Establishment cultivator kills a Qi Refining cultivator effortlessly. The gap is insurmountable without an extraordinary special advantage.
- **Minor-realm gaps**: Early-stage cultivators struggle against late-stage opponents, but equipment/talismans/formations can compensate.
- **Swarm tactics**: No number of low-level cultivators can overwhelm a high-level cultivator because area-of-effect abilities exist.

## 5. Outline Structure (Standard: 2 Million Chinese Characters)

### Volume One: Sect Turmoil (Chapters 1-100)
- **Starting area**: Competition among outer disciples before Foundation Establishment.
- **Central conflict**: Resource struggles and malicious framing.
- **Climax**: Win the sect tournament and establish the foundation.

### Volume Two: Bloody Trials (Chapters 101-250)
- **Setting**: Secret realms/ruins.
- **Central conflict**: Battles among sects and demonic invasion.
- **Growth**: Form a golden core and acquire a life-bound artifact.

### Volume Three: Overseas/Central Continent (Chapters 251-500)
- **Setting change**: Escape pursuit or travel voluntarily.
- **Central conflict**: The hardships of independent cultivation and meeting new companions.
- **Climax**: Form a nascent soul and establish a faction.

### Volume Four: War Between Realms (Chapters 501-1000)
- **Scope**: War between humans and demons, or righteous and demonic factions.
- **Status**: Become a regional power capable of deciding the war.
