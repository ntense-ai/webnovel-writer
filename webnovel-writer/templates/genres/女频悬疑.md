# Women-Oriented Mystery Template

> Runtime preset identifier: `女频悬疑` (preserved for taxonomy and initialization compatibility).

> **Core appeal**: Logical deduction + human emotion + relationship reversals. Use sensitive observation to uncover truth within the fog of intimate relationships.

- Recommended constraint pack: Pack F13
- General overlay: Pack U02

## 1. Core Subgenres

### Emotional Mystery
- **Features**: Mystery centered on emotional relationships.
- **Core payoffs**: Relationship truths, collapsed trust, emotional reversals.
- **Representative works**: *Lost in the Stars*, *Imperfect Victim*, *The Returning Daughter*.

### Family Mystery
- **Features**: Secrets and lies within a family.
- **Core payoffs**: Family secrets, mysterious origins, reversals of familial affection.
- **Representative works**: *The Bad Kids*, *A Little Mood for Love*, *All Is Well*.

### Workplace Mystery
- **Features**: Conspiracies and truth in professional settings.
- **Core payoffs**: Workplace plots, revelations, comeback and revenge.
- **Representative works**: *Psychologist*, *Draw the Line*, *The Tale of Rose*.

### Revenge and Deduction
- **Features**: Approach others with a purpose and uncover the past.
- **Core payoffs**: Successful revenge, truth revealed, justice served.
- **Representative works**: *The Murder in Kairoutei*, *The Pavilion*, *The Crack of Dawn*.

## 3. Mystery Design

### Puzzle Types
| Type | Central question | Revelation method |
|------|----------|----------|
| Identity | Who are they? | Peel away layers |
| Motive | Why do this? | Emotional revelation |
| Relationship | What is the real relationship? | Reversal |
| Past | What happened? | Reconstruct the truth |

### Planting Clues
```
Layer One: Appearances
- Seemingly harmonious relationships.
- Apparently normal routines.
- A faint sense of something wrong.

Layer Two: Doubts
- Implausible details.
- Contradictory statements.
- Hidden emotions.

Layer Three: Truth
- The relationship's real face.
- The hidden past.
- The true motive.
```

### Reversal Design
| Type | Effect | Required setup |
|----------|------|----------|
| Identity reversal | Shock | Sufficient foreshadowing |
| Relationship reversal | Heartbreak | Emotional groundwork |
| Motive reversal | Understanding | Background explanation |
| Ending reversal | Surprise | Consistent logic |

## 4. Classic Payoff Patterns

### Truth Revealed
- **Scene**: Truth emerges from layers of mystery.
- **Payoff**: Realization connects earlier and later details.
- **Key**: Foreshadowing and coherent logic.

### Relationship Reversal
- **Scene**: The most trusted person is an enemy.
- **Payoff**: Shock, heartbreak, anger.
- **Key**: Sufficient emotional groundwork.

### Successful Revenge
- **Scene**: Retaliate against someone who caused harm.
- **Payoff**: Justice and vindication.
- **Key**: Maintain moral boundaries.

### Self-Redemption
- **Scene**: Escape a predicament and begin anew.
- **Payoff**: Transformation, independence, strength.
- **Key**: Show the growth process.

## 8. Common Pitfalls

### Must Avoid
- ❌ Emotion without deduction.
- ❌ Unfair clues.
- ❌ Overly contrived reversals.
- ❌ An excessively naive heroine.

### Recommended Practices
- ✅ Advance emotion and deduction together.
- ✅ Fair, traceable clues.
- ✅ Foreshadowed reversals.
- ✅ An intelligent heroine who grows.
