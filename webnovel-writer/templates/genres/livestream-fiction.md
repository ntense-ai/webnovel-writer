# Livestreaming Fiction Template

> Runtime preset identifier: `livestream-fiction` (English taxonomy and initialization preset).

> **Core appeal**: Real-time interaction + reversals of public opinion + monetization. Audience feedback drives immediate narrative satisfaction.

## 1. Core Subgenres

### Livestream-Commerce Rise
- **Features**: Grow from a small streamer into a leading creator through product knowledge and persuasive presentation.
- **Core payoffs**: Huge sales in one stream, winning performance bets, brands begging to collaborate.
- **Best for**: Urban growth stories and realistic entrepreneurship.

### Talent Competition
- **Features**: Competition in singing/dancing/instruments/public speaking.
- **Core payoffs**: Legendary live performances, platform-wide trending topics, silencing doubters.
- **Best for**: Light pacing and strong interaction.

### On-Site Reviews and Documentary Streams
- **Features**: Visiting businesses offline and exposing industry secrets.
- **Core payoffs**: Authenticity tests, fighting for consumer rights, reversing public opinion.
- **Best for**: Realistic fiction and social issues.

### Wealthy Donor Gift Battles
- **Features**: Gift rankings, supporter rankings, and battles between livestream rooms.
- **Core payoffs**: Ranking sprints, head-to-head comebacks, revealing a hidden patron's identity.
- **Best for**: High-intensity wish fulfillment and heightened emotions.

## 2. Worldbuilding: Platform Ecosystem

### Traffic Stages
| Stage | Features | Source of conflict |
|------|------|----------|
| Cold start | Few recommendations, little exposure | Anxiety about launching an account |
| Growth | Steady audience expansion | Homogeneous content |
| Breakout | Surging traffic | Haters and manipulated narratives |
| Top tier | Deep commercialization | Contracts, public opinion, team management |

### Key Roles
- **Streamer**: Delivers the core content and embodies the public persona.
- **Operations/editor**: Amplifies traffic.
- **Brand**: Commercial collaboration and negotiating leverage.
- **Audience**: Interactions among supporters, casual viewers, and haters.

---

## 3. Classic Payoff Patterns

### Turning a Live Disaster Around
- **Scene**: A rival orchestrates a smear campaign; the livestream narrative spirals out of control.
- **Payoff**: Evidence revealed live reverses the situation and changes the internet's verdict.
- **Key**: Plant the evidence chain beforehand.

### Reaching the Top of the Rankings
- **Scene**: Trailing with five minutes left in a head-to-head contest.
- **Payoff**: Strategic vote gathering and decisive support produce a last-second comeback.
- **Key**: Build peaks in the sequence rather than one uninterrupted rout.

### Negotiating with Brands
- **Scene**: A major brand cuts its offer or shifts blame.
- **Payoff**: The streamer counters with data and reputation, gaining negotiating leverage.
- **Key**: Keep the business logic realistic.

### Account Rebirth
- **Scene**: Returning after an account ban or reputation collapse.
- **Payoff**: A new account breaks through the cold start and reaches the top again.
- **Key**: The comeback needs differentiated positioning.

## 4. Outline Structure (Reference: 1.5 Million Chinese Characters)

### Volume One: Launching the Account (Chapters 1-60)
- **Core**: Define the account's positioning and win the first stable audience.
- **Climax**: A modest breakout stream.

### Volume Two: Breaking Out (Chapters 61-160)
- **Core**: Upgrade content, gain platform recommendations, and enter a competitive niche.
- **Climax**: A ranking comeback.

### Volume Three: Attack and Defense (Chapters 161-300)
- **Core**: Handle haters, public-relations crises, and internal team conflict.
- **Climax**: A battle that reverses public opinion.

### Volume Four: Monetization (Chapters 301-460)
- **Core**: Brand collaborations, supply chains, and team expansion.
- **Climax**: Win a commercial performance bet.

### Volume Five: A New Stage (Chapters 461-620)
- **Core**: Move from an individual streamer to a content company.
- **Climax**: Achieve legendary success in a new niche.

## 5. Common Pitfalls

### Must Avoid
- ❌ An unrealistic ecosystem where all statistics come from miraculous traffic.
- ❌ Audiences serving only as background rather than driving the plot.
- ❌ Arguments every chapter without career progress.
- ❌ Public-opinion reversals without evidence or setup.

### Recommended Practices
- ✅ Show audience feedback affecting a decision at least once per chapter.
- ✅ Complete the loop before, during, and after each livestream.
- ✅ Plant 2-3 evidence points before key reversals and pay them off later.
- ✅ Advance business conflicts alongside emotional character arcs.
