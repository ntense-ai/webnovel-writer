# Ancient Historical Fiction Template

> Runtime preset identifier: `ancient-history` (English taxonomy and initialization preset).

> **Core appeal**: Sweeping historical context + political maneuvering + expanding scope. Tell personal legends and changing eras within history's currents.

- Recommended constraint pack: Pack M08
- General overlay: Pack U01

## 1. Core Subgenres

### Political Struggle for Supremacy
- **Features**: Competing for the realm in troubled times.
- **Core payoffs**: Strategic dominance, recruiting talent, unification.
- **Representative works**: *Overturning Han*, *The Most Distinguished of the Three Kingdoms*, *Han Homeland*, *Qin Official*.

### Official Career Advancement
- **Features**: Bureaucratic ups and downs and successive promotions.
- **Core payoffs**: Political contests, promotions that silence rivals, overwhelming court influence.
- **Representative works**: *First-Rank Official*, *Governing All Under Heaven*, *Marquis of the Great Ming*, *A Gentleman of Humble Origins*.

### Technological Development
- **Features**: Modern knowledge changes history.
- **Core payoffs**: Technological superiority, domestic development, a wealthy nation and strong army.
- **Representative works**: *New Song*, *Tang Brick*, *My Heroic Husband*, *Return to the Ming Dynasty as a Prince*.

### Military Campaigns
- **Features**: Battlefield campaigns and the road to becoming a celebrated general.
- **Core payoffs**: Tactical superiority, defeating greater numbers, legendary frontier victories.
- **Representative works**: *Twin Dragons of the Tang*, *Stealing the Ming*, *Those Things About the Ming Dynasty*.

## 3. Bureaucratic System Design

### Promotion Routes
```
Examination route:
Candidate → Licentiate → Provincial graduate → Metropolitan graduate → Hanlin Academy → Six Ministries → Grand Secretariat

Military-merit route:
Soldier → Squad leader → Hundred-household commander → Thousand-household commander → General → Grand general

Hereditary-privilege route:
Patronage appointment → Honorary office → Substantive office → Key office
```

### Political Resources
| Resource | Description | Acquisition |
|------|------|----------|
| Connections | Examination peers, hometown ties, teachers/students | Examinations/socializing |
| Reputation | Integrity/administrative achievements | Actions/publicity |
| Faction | Political affiliation | Alignment/cultivation |
| Wealth | Economic foundation | Salary/business |
| Military command | Armed backing | Merit/appointment |

### Court Maneuvering
```
Information war (30%):
- Gather intelligence.
- Shape public opinion.
- Suppress information.

Personnel war (40%):
- Recruit allies.
- Attack opponents.
- Install confidants.

Policy war (30%):
- Propose policies.
- Obstruct rivals.

```

## 4. Classic Payoff Patterns

### Examination Comeback
- **Scene**: A scholar of humble origins succeeds in the imperial examinations.
- **Payoff**: Silences the powerful and becomes famous overnight.
- **Key**: Establish effort and preparation.

### Battlefield Legend
- **Scene**: Win a crucial battle while outnumbered.
- **Payoff**: Tactical dominance brings nationwide fame.
- **Example**: Defeat a powerful enemy with an ingenious strategy.

### Courtroom Reversal
- **Scene**: Counterattack after impeachment/framing.
- **Payoff**: Conclusive evidence brings down the opponent.
- **Key**: Use planning rather than excessive coincidence.

### Recruiting Talent
- **Scene**: Historical celebrities join the protagonist.
- **Payoff**: A galaxy of talented followers gathers around them.
- **Key**: Provide a convincing reason to follow.

## 5. Outline Structure (Standard: 2 Million Chinese Characters)

### Volume One: Entering the Game (Chapters 1-80)
- **Core**: Enter the historical stage.
- **Status**: Bottom tier/periphery.
- **Climax**: First display of ability establishes a foothold.

### Volume Two: Rise (Chapters 81-200)
- **Core**: Accumulate strength and expand influence.
- **Status**: Mid-level official/local power.
- **Climax**: Gain an important office/territory.

### Volume Three: Contention (Chapters 201-400)
- **Core**: Confront the main rival directly.
- **Status**: Regional dignitary/warlord.
- **Climax**: Defeat the main rival.

### Volume Four: Supremacy (Chapters 401-600)
- **Core**: Compete for the realm.
- **Status**: Top-tier faction.
- **Climax**: Decisive battle/coup.

### Volume Five: Establishing the Realm (Chapters 601-700)
- **Core**: Unification/reform and a new order.
- **Status**: Supreme authority.
- **Climax**: Satisfying resolution.

## 7. Creating Historical Atmosphere

### Language
- Use classical vocabulary without obscurity.
- Keep official titles accurate.
- Avoid modern internet slang.

### Researching Details
| Area | Key point | Pitfall |
|------|------|------|
| Clothing | Fits the dynasty | Misattributed styles |
| Food | Plausible ingredients | Anachronistic foods |
| Etiquette | Clear hierarchy | Inappropriate disregard of ritual |
| Forms of address | Accurate offices | Misused titles |

### Using Major Events
- Let historical events drive the plot.
- Let the protagonist participate without displacing everything else.
- Preserve broad historical trends unless writing alternate history.

---

## 8. Common Pitfalls

### Must Avoid
- ❌ Numerous serious historical errors.
- ❌ Excessively modern thinking.
- ❌ Advancement that is too quick and easy.
- ❌ Tool-like supporting characters.

### Recommended Practices
- ✅ Accurate basic history.
- ✅ Era-appropriate thinking.
- ✅ Setbacks and costs.
- ✅ Fully realized supporting characters.
