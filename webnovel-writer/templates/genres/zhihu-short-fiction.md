# Zhihu-Style Short Fiction Template

> Runtime preset identifier: `zhihu-short-fiction` (English taxonomy and initialization preset).

> **Core appeal**: Short and fast + intense conflict + dramatic reversals. Complete a full narrative arc in 3,000-15,000 Chinese characters; the first 300 decide whether readers stay.

---

## Recommended Creative Constraints (Optional)
- Recommended constraint pack: Pack M17
- General overlay: Pack U02

## 1. Core Characteristics

### Length Positioning
| Type | Chinese characters | Suitable use |
|------|------|----------|
| Micro-short | 3,000-5,000 | Single reversal, emotional release |
| Standard short | 5,000-10,000 | Double reversal, complete arc |
| Longer short | 10,000-15,000 | Multiple reversals, complex emotions |

### Golden 300-Character Rule
- **First 50**: Create suspense or conflict.
- **50-150**: Establish the character's predicament.
- **150-300**: Present the central conflict.

## 2. Ten Opening-Hook Templates

### 1. Opening with Death
```
Template: I died, and then [reversal].
Example: I died on our wedding anniversary. The irony was that my killer cried the hardest at my funeral.
Suitable for: Revenge, mystery, anguished fiction.
```

### 2. Reverse Chronology
```
Template: [Outcome]; it all began at [time].
Example: When he knelt before me begging to reunite, I could only laugh. Three years ago, he wore a very different expression.
Suitable for: Redemption-and-pursuit arcs, reconciliation.
```

### 3. Identity Reversal
```
Template: Everyone thought I was [A], but I was actually [B].
Example: Everyone thought I was the poor relative reaching above her station, including the cousin who had "kindly taken me in."
Suitable for: Humiliation reversals, wish fulfillment, wealthy families.
```

### 4. Extreme Predicament
```
Template: [Extreme situation], and I [unexpected reaction].
Example: Trapped in a cold-storage room at minus thirty degrees, I smiled instead. At last, I could die with a reason nobody could question.
Suitable for: Anguish, redemption, mystery.
```

### 5. Dialogue Opening
```
Template: "[Shocking line]"
Example: "Sign it. I'll raise the child. You leave with nothing." He pushed the divorce agreement toward me, his eyes full of disgust.
Suitable for: Marriage stories, anguish, wish fulfillment.
```

### 6. Rebirth/Transmigration
```
Template: I opened my eyes and returned to [crucial moment].
Example: I opened my eyes on the day before I was pushed downstairs. This time, I would not be the one to die.
Suitable for: Rebirth and revenge, entering a novel.
```

### 7. Secret Revealed
```
Template: What [person] did not know was [secret].
Example: What my husband did not know was that I had hired his idealized former love to play a part.
Suitable for: Reversals, revenge, wish fulfillment.
```

### 8. Numerical Shock
```
Template: [Number] + [abnormal fact].
Example: In seven years of marriage, he sent me to the emergency room thirty-two times. I finally learned one thing: how to play dead.
Suitable for: Anguish, awakening, revenge.
```

### 9. Contrast
```
Template: Once [state A], now [state B].
Example: Once, he was a god far beyond my reach. Now he knelt before me, begging me to look at him.
Suitable for: Comebacks, romantic pursuit.
```

### 10. Suspenseful Question
```
Template: Do you know [question]?
Example: Do you know how desperate a person can become? Perhaps it is when they no longer have even the strength to die.
Suitable for: Anguished and emotional fiction.
```

## 3. Classic Genre Templates

### Painful Redemption and Pursuit (8,000 Chinese Characters)
```
Structure: Outcome → Memories → Turning point → Pursuit → Ending

Act One (1,000): He humbly begs to reunite; she is cold.
Act Two (3,000): Flash back to marital mistreatment/neglect.
Act Three (1,500): The catalyst for her departure.
Act Four (2,000): His efforts to win her back.
Act Five (500): Happy or tragic ending.
```

### Rebirth and Revenge (6,000 Chinese Characters)
```
Structure: Tragic death → Rebirth → Planning → Counterattack → Ending

Act One (800): Tragic death in the previous life.
Act Two (500): Rebirth and a vow.
Act Three (2,500): Change fate through careful strategy.
Act Four (1,700): Crucial counterattack and revelation.
Act Five (500): Enemies' fates plus a new life.
```

### Wealthy-Family Humiliation Reversal (5,000 Chinese Characters)
```
Structure: Bullied → Endures → Identity exposed → Turns the tables → Resolution

Act One (800): Dismissed/bullied.
Act Two (1,200): Endurance accumulates emotion.
Act Three (1,500): Identity revelation unfolds.
Act Four (1,200): Climactic humiliation of detractors.
Act Five (300): Reactions plus ending.
```

## 4. Ending Patterns

### HE (Happy Ending)
```
Suitable for: Sweet romance, successful pursuit, fulfilled revenge.
Ending patterns:
- Reconciliation and embrace.
- Wedding/children.
- Happy daily life years later.
```

### BE (Bad Ending)
```
Suitable for: Anguished fiction, poignant regret, emotional heartbreak.
Ending patterns:
- Missing each other for life.
- One partner dies.
- Unattainable love.
- Open-ended tragedy.
```

### Open Ending
```
Suitable for: Lingering meaning and discussion.
Ending patterns:
- Leave suspense.
- Let readers imagine.
- Lasting resonance.
```

## 5. Pacing Control

### Golden Length Allocation
```
Opening hook: 5% (capture readers).
Background: 15% (quick immersion).
Escalation: 40% (core content).
Climactic reversal: 25% (emotional explosion).
Resolution: 15% (lingering resonance).
```

### Acceleration Techniques
- **Short sentences**: Create tension.
- **Dialogue-driven action**: Accelerate the plot.
- **Omit transitions**: Jump directly to crucial scenes.
- **Emotional vocabulary**: Strengthen impact.

### Avoid
- ❌ Long background exposition.
- ❌ Excessive inner monologue.
- ❌ Dragging transitional scenes.
- ❌ Setup that takes too long to pay off.

## 6. Emotional Curves

### Anguished Fiction
```
Opening (low) → Sweet memories (high) → Suffering (sustained low) → Greatest anguish (bottom) → Ending (HE rises/BE falls further)
```

### Wish-Fulfillment Fiction
```
Opening (suppressed) → Endurance (accumulation) → Explosion (high) → Humiliation reversal (higher) → Resolution (satisfaction)
```

### Reversal Fiction
```
Opening (false appearance A) → Setup (reinforces A) → Reversal (B revealed) → Shock (impact) → New understanding (deeper meaning)
```

## 7. Rapid Character Construction

### Three-Second Character Method
```
Define a character in one sentence:
- Occupation/identity + core trait + key conflict.

Examples:
- "An apparently submissive daughter-in-law who holds leverage over the entire wealthy family."
- "The ex-wife abandoned by a scoundrel is secretly a powerful figure."
- "The CEO now begging to win her back drove her away himself three years ago."
```

### Characterization Through Contrast
```
Early versus late:
- Humble early → queen later.
- Cold early → humble later.
- Glorious early → destitute later.
```
