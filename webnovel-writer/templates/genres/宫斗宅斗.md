# Palace and Household Intrigue Template

> Runtime preset identifier: `宫斗宅斗` (preserved for taxonomy and initialization compatibility).

> **Core appeal**: Rule-based contests + battles of cunning + leaps in status. Advance carefully within great estates and rewrite fate through intelligence.

## 1. Core Subgenres

### Competition for Imperial Favor
- **Features**: Palace consorts compete for the emperor's affection.
- **Core payoffs**: Outsmart rivals, gain favor, rise in status.
- **Representative works**: *Empresses in the Palace*, *Ruyi's Royal Love in the Palace*, *Story of Yanxi Palace*.

### Household Infighting
- **Features**: Great-family conflict and legitimate/concubine-born rivalries.
- **Core payoffs**: Counter framing, gain family authority, successful revenge.
- **Representative works**: *The Story of Minglan*, *The Sword and the Brocade*.

### Political Palace Intrigue
- **Features**: Court politics and power struggles.
- **Core payoffs**: Political planning, defeating opponents, dominating the court.
- **Representative works**: *Nirvana in Fire*, *Joy of Life*.

### Rebirth and Revenge
- **Features**: Return to the past and change a tragic fate.
- **Core payoffs**: Foresight, revenge, reversing one's life.
- **Representative works**: *Rebirth of the Malicious Imperial Consort*, *The Concubine's Daughter's Strategy*.

## 2. Worldbuilding: Palace and Household Society

### Imperial Consort Hierarchy (Qing Model)
| Rank | Title | Number | Authority |
|------|------|------|------|
| First | Empress | 1 | Heads the inner palace |
| Second | Imperial noble consort | 1 | Deputy empress |
| Third | Noble consort | 2 | High-ranking consort |
| Fourth | Consort | 4 | Heads a palace |
| Fifth | Imperial concubine | 6 | Holds a formal title |
| Sixth | Noble lady | Unlimited | Low rank |
| Seventh | First/second attendant | Unlimited | Lowest rank |

### Household Hierarchy
| Identity | Status | Authority |
|------|------|------|
| Dowager matriarch | Highest | Her word is decisive |
| Household mistress | Manager | Controls domestic affairs |
| Legitimate son/daughter | Primary lineage | Inheritance rights |
| Concubine-born son/daughter | Secondary | Limited rights |
| Concubine/chamber attendant | Low | Dependent on the mistress |
| Maid/servant | Lowest | No authority |

### Resource System
- **Favor**: The central resource determining status.
- **Money**: Cultivates relationships and buys loyalty.
- **Connections**: Networks within palace/estate.
- **Intelligence**: Essential to knowing oneself and the enemy.
- **Children**: The strongest bargaining chip.

## 3. Intrigue Methods

### Overt Methods
| Method | Features | Risk |
|------|------|------|
| Competing for favor | Direct competition | Makes enemies |
| Complaining to authority | Uses another's power | Backlash |
| Alliance | Safety in numbers | Betrayal |
| Feigned weakness | Hides strength | Bullying |

### Covert Methods
| Method | Features | Risk |
|------|------|------|
| Framing | Plants blame | Exposure |
| Drugging/poisoning | Secret harm | Discovery |
| Bribery | Turns the opponent's people | Double agents |
| Rumors | Reputational attack | Backlash |

### Defensive Methods
- **Poison testing**: Prevent poisoning.
- **Informants**: Watch enemy movements.
- **Patrons**: Find protection.
- **Leverage**: Know the opponent's weaknesses.

## 4. Classic Payoff Patterns

### Countering a Frame-Up
- **Scene**: The protagonist is framed with apparently conclusive evidence.
- **Reversal**: Prior preparation turns the tables.
- **Key**: Foreshadow the counterattack; no unearned reversal.

### Outsmarting Public Humiliation
- **Scene**: An opponent creates difficulties publicly.
- **Payoff**: A clever response leaves the opponent embarrassed.
- **Key**: Stay within the protagonist's status and social boundaries.

### Leap in Status
- **Scene**: Rise from a low to a high position.
- **Payoff**: Former detractors must bow.
- **Key**: Establish grounds for promotion; avoid abruptness.

### Successful Revenge
- **Scene**: Retaliate against someone who caused harm.
- **Payoff**: The opponent experiences the protagonist's former suffering.
- **Key**: Keep revenge proportionate, not excessively cruel.

## 5. Outline Structure (Standard: 1 Million Chinese Characters)

### Volume One: Entering the Game (Chapters 1-50)
- **Core**: Enter palace/estate and learn the rules.
- **Status**: Low rank.
- **Climax**: Resolve the first crisis and gain a foothold.

### Volume Two: A Foothold (Chapters 51-120)
- **Core**: Build a network and initial status.
- **Status**: Lower-middle rank.
- **Climax**: Defeat the first opponent and earn a small promotion.

### Volume Three: Rise (Chapters 121-200)
- **Core**: Enter the central struggle.
- **Status**: Middle rank.
- **Climax**: Bring down the main opponent and rise substantially.

### Volume Four: Pinnacle (Chapters 201-280)
- **Core**: Compete for the highest position.
- **Status**: High rank.
- **Climax**: Become the final winner.

### Volume Five: Resolution (Chapters 281-350)
- **Core**: Resolve remaining issues and conclude.
- **Status**: Highest rank.
- **Climax**: Satisfying resolution.

## 6. Relationship Design

### Core Relationships
| Relationship | Function | Development |
|------|------|------|
| Male lead | Romance | Strangers to lovers |
| Close female friend | Assistance | Possible betrayal |
| Opponent | Conflict | Enemy to friend/complete defeat |
| Elder | Patron/obstacle | Earn acceptance |
| Servant | Intelligence/execution | Cultivate loyalty |

### Opponent Design
| Type | Features | Outcome |
|------|------|------|
| Foolish and malicious | Arrogant and overbearing | Humbled early |
| Calculating | Outwardly kind | Exposed mid-story |
| Powerful enemy | Formidable resources | Defeated late |
| Tragic opponent | Has understandable reasons | Possible reconciliation |

## 7. Palace/Household Rules

### Core Rules
- **Hierarchy**: Lower-ranking people cannot offend superiors.
- **Lineage distinctions**: Legitimate children outrank concubine-born children.
- **Maternal status through children**: Offspring stabilize a mother's position.
- **Favor rules**: The favored person's words carry weight.

### Taboo Boundaries
- **Killing one's master**: A capital offense.
- **Adultery**: An affair with an outside man is a capital offense.
- **Rebellion**: Participation implicates extended kin.
- **Sorcery**: Witchcraft is a grave taboo.

---

## 8. Common Pitfalls

### Must Avoid
- ❌ Every opponent is foolish.
- ❌ Too many protagonist cheats.
- ❌ Unthinking devotion from the male lead.
- ❌ Repetitive intrigue methods.

### Recommended Practices
- ✅ Intelligent opponents.
- ✅ The protagonist wins through wit.
- ✅ Romance includes setbacks.
- ✅ Varied methods of conflict.
