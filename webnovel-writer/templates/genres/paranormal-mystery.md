# Supernatural Mystery Template

> Runtime preset identifier: `paranormal-mystery` (English taxonomy and initialization preset).

> **Core appeal**: Oppressive atmosphere + uncanny rules + logical consistency. Explore unknown truths on the boundary between fear and reason.

## 1. Core Subgenres

### Paranormal Exploration
- **Features**: Explore haunted locations and uncover uncanny truths.
- **Core payoffs**: Horror atmosphere, revelations, desperate escapes.
- **Representative works**: *My House of Horrors*, *Strange Records of the Civil Investigation Bureau*, *The Grave Robbers' Chronicles*.

### Urban Legends
- **Features**: Supernatural events in modern cities.
- **Core payoffs**: Horror close to home, discovering rules, outsmarting anomalies.
- **Representative works**: *I Learned to Slay Gods in a Mental Hospital*, *Lord of Mysteries*.

### Exorcism
- **Features**: A protagonist with special powers handles paranormal incidents.
- **Core payoffs**: Growing abilities, exorcism battles, saving others.
- **Representative works**: *Descendants of Maoshan*, *My Years as a Taoist Priest*.

### Rule-Based Horror
- **Features**: Survival requires following rules.
- **Core payoffs**: Discovering, exploiting, and turning rules against threats.
- **Representative works**: *Return from the Abyss*, *Rule-Based Horror*.

## 2. Worldbuilding: Supernatural Society

### Supernatural Ranks
| Rank | Features | Danger |
|------|------|--------|
| Ordinary phenomenon | Harmless/minor harassment | Low |
| Evil spirit | Aggressive | Medium |
| Vengeful ghost | Extremely dangerous | High |
| Anomaly | Rule-level being | Very high |
| Forbidden entity | Indescribable | Lethal |

### Supernatural Origins
- **Resentment**: Concentrated intense negative emotions.
- **Curses**: Deliberately inflicted malice.
- **Taboos**: Violating a particular rule.
- **Other worlds**: Coming from another dimension.
- **Nature**: Anomalies between heaven and earth.

### Faction Landscape
- **Official agencies**: Special departments handling paranormal incidents.
- **Civilian organizations**: Taoists, exorcists, mediums.
- **Supernatural beings**: Ghosts, anomalies, gods.
- **Ordinary people**: The uninformed majority.

## 3. Horror Atmosphere Design

### Horror Types
| Type | Features | Techniques |
|------|------|------|
| Psychological | Suggestion-led | Omission, imagination |
| Visual | Direct description | Gore, deformity |
| Atmospheric | Environmental mood | Oppression, uncanniness |
| Rule-based | Violation means death | Tension, anxiety |

### Horror Pacing
```
Setup (20%):
- Anomalies within everyday life.
- Ominous signs.
- Increasingly oppressive atmosphere.

Escalation (40%):
- Paranormal incidents occur.
- Horror intensifies.
- Someone meets a terrible fate.

Climax (30%):
- Confront the core horror.
- Life hangs by a thread.
- Reveal the truth.

Resolution (10%):
- Horror ends/continues.
- Lingering resonance/foreshadowing.
```

## 4. Classic Payoff Patterns

### Decoding Rules
- **Scene**: Discover an anomaly's rules.
- **Payoff**: Exploit loopholes to escape/defeat it.
- **Key**: Rules must be consistent and solutions plausible.

### Revealing Truth
- **Scene**: Truth emerges from layers of mystery.
- **Payoff**: A moment of realization connects earlier and later details.
- **Key**: Foreshadow the answer; never invent it at the last moment.

### Desperate Escape
- **Scene**: Trapped in a supernatural space.
- **Payoff**: A narrow, thrilling escape.
- **Key**: Escape must cost something and cannot be easy.

### Ability Awakening
- **Scene**: A special ability awakens at the brink of death.
- **Payoff**: Turn the tables on the supernatural threat.
- **Key**: Abilities require limits.

## 6. Rule Design

### Rule Types
| Type | Features | Example |
|------|------|------|
| Prohibition | Must not do something | Do not look back |
| Obligation | Must do something | Must smile |
| Conditional | Triggers under conditions | Seeing it means death |
| Time-bound | Applies at a particular time | Three in the morning |

### Design Principles
- **Consistency**: No internal contradictions.
- **Verifiability**: Violations have clear consequences.
- **Loopholes**: Rules can be exploited.
- **Origins**: There is a reason the rules formed.

## 7. Supernatural Ability Design

### Ability Types
| Type | Features | Cost |
|------|------|------|
| Spirit sight | Sees supernatural beings | They can see the user |
| Mediumship | Communicates with spirits | Mental expenditure |
| Exorcism | Expels supernatural beings | Physical expenditure |
| Sealing | Seals supernatural beings | Materials |
| Foresight | Foresees danger | Headaches/bleeding |

### Ability Limits
- **Usage count**: Limited daily uses.
- **Side effects**: Weakness afterward.
- **Conditions**: Specific prerequisites.
- **Rank**: Ineffective against higher-level beings.

---

## 8. Common Pitfalls

### Must Avoid
- ❌ Piling on horror without logic.
- ❌ Arbitrary rule loopholes.
- ❌ Protagonist cheats destroying the sense of pressure.
- ❌ Contradictory supernatural lore.

### Recommended Practices
- ✅ Give horror logic and setup.
- ✅ Keep rules consistent and verifiable.
- ✅ Give the protagonist costs and limits.
- ✅ Make the setting detailed and consistent.
