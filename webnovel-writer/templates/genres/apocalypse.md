# Apocalypse Fiction Template

> Runtime preset identifier: `apocalypse` (English taxonomy and initialization preset).

> **Core appeal**: Resource collapse + survival struggles + moral choices. Rebuild order amid civilization's ruins and find hope in despair.

---

## Recommended Creative Constraints (Optional)
- Recommended constraint pack: Pack M20
- General overlay: Pack U03

## 1. Core Subgenres

### Zombie Apocalypse
- **Features**: A viral outbreak resurrects the dead; humanity barely survives.
- **Core payoffs**: Leveling up by killing zombies, building safe zones, rescuing survivors.
- **Representative works**: *Dark Summoner of the Apocalypse*, *Apocalypse Super System*, *I Have a Doomsday City*.

### Mutation Apocalypse
- **Features**: Spiritual awakening/radiation causes mutations; plants and animals evolve while humans awaken powers.
- **Core payoffs**: Awakening powers, hunting mutated beasts, collecting evolution materials.
- **Representative works**: *Abyss Summoner of the Apocalypse*, *Global Evolution*, *My Apocalypse Base Vehicle*.

### Natural-Disaster Apocalypse
- **Features**: Natural disasters (ice ages/droughts/earthquakes) create extreme scarcity.
- **Core payoffs**: Stockpiling resources, managing shelters, exploring ruins.
- **Representative works**: *Apocalypse Cockroach*, *I Am the Ruler of the Apocalypse*.

### Rebuilding Order
- **Features**: Post-disaster reconstruction, factional competition, creating a new civilization.
- **Core payoffs**: Expanding influence, recruiting talent, building institutions.
- **Representative works**: *Rebirth: A New Apocalyptic Order*, *Dark Derivatives of the Apocalypse*.

## 2. Worldbuilding: Apocalypse Sociology

### Resource Rules
- **Food**: The hardest currency; people kill for a meal.
- **Water**: Clean water is worth more than gold.
- **Medicine**: A bottle of antibiotics can buy a life.
- **Fuel**: Control fuel and you control mobility.

### The Human Spectrum
- **Saints**: Maintain principles and protect the weak (rare).
- **Ordinary people**: Follow the crowd and do good when possible.
- **Moral gray zone**: Will do wrong to survive.
- **Villains**: Exploit chaos; prey on the weak.

### Faction Types
| Type | Features | Relationship to protagonist |
|------|------|-----------|
| Military remnants | Organized and disciplined | Cooperation/opposition |
| Survivor settlements | Safety in numbers | Join/found one |
| Raiders | Live by plunder | Main enemies |
| Cults | Apocalyptic faith | Mid-to-late-story bosses |
| Research institutions | Seek a cure/the truth | Quest sources |

## 3. Powers/Evolution System

### Awakening Tiers
- **Tier 1**: Physical enhancement; 2-3 times stronger than an ordinary person.
- **Tier 2**: Initial elemental powers; controls one element.
- **Tier 3**: Elemental mastery and area-of-effect attacks.
- **Tier 4**: Forms a domain that distorts local rules.
- **Tier 5**: Legendary; can eliminate a city's zombies single-handedly.

### Power Categories
| Type | Features | Rarity |
|------|------|--------|
| Strength | Overwhelming brute force | Common |
| Speed | Mobility and agility | Common |
| Elemental | Fire/ice/lightning/wind | Uncommon |
| Mental | Control/perception | Rare |
| Spatial | Storage/teleportation | Extremely rare |
| Healing | Repairs injuries | Extremely rare |

### Evolution Materials
- **Crystal cores**: Harvested from mutated creatures, with corresponding tiers.
- **Evolution potions**: Produced by research institutions; both risky and rewarding.
- **Special foods**: Fruit from mutated plants, with a chance of awakening powers.

## 4. Classic Payoff Patterns

### Stockpiling Supplies
- **Scene**: Before or early in the apocalypse, the protagonist hoards supplies.
- **Payoff**: Others go hungry while the protagonist eats lavishly.
- **Key**: Provide a plausible reason for preparation (rebirth/foresight/spatial storage).

### Base Building
- **Scene**: Find land and build from scratch.
- **Payoff**: From wreckage to fortress, from solitary survivor to team.
- **Key**: Show the process to create a sense of accomplishment.

### Rescue and Recruitment
- **Scene**: Rescue survivors who have nowhere to turn.
- **Payoff**: Gain loyal followers and expand the team.
- **Key**: Rescued people must contribute skills/resources/connections.

### Overwhelming a Faction
- **Scene**: A minor faction provokes the protagonist without understanding their strength.
- **Payoff**: One person destroys a gang and deters everyone nearby.
- **Key**: Establish sufficient groundwork; avoid mindless domination.

## 5. Outline Structure (Standard: 2 Million Chinese Characters)

### Volume One: Survival (Chapters 1-80)
- **Core**: Apocalypse arrives; the protagonist awakens and struggles to survive.
- **Setting**: City ruins.
- **Climax**: First high-tier mutant kill grants a crucial ability.

### Volume Two: A Foothold (Chapters 81-180)
- **Core**: Establish/join a base and gain stability.
- **Setting**: Around the base.
- **Climax**: Repel a massive zombie horde/hostile faction.

### Volume Three: Expansion (Chapters 181-350)
- **Core**: Expand influence and absorb neighboring groups.
- **Setting**: Regional.
- **Climax**: Become the regional hegemon.

### Volume Four: Struggle for Supremacy (Chapters 351-550)
- **Core**: Compete with other major factions.
- **Setting**: Across regions.
- **Climax**: Unify a major region/discover the apocalypse's truth.

### Volume Five: Endgame (Chapters 551-700)
- **Core**: Resolve the apocalypse's cause/establish a new order.
- **Setting**: Nationwide/global.
- **Climax**: Humanity regains hope.

## 6. Team Composition

### Core Team
| Character | Role | Ability | Function |
|------|------|------|------|
| Protagonist | Leader | Versatile/special | Central decision-maker |
| Warrior | Damage dealer | Strength/elemental | Frontline combat |
| Assassin | Scout | Speed/stealth | Intelligence gathering |
| Medic | Support | Healing | Team sustain |
| Logistics specialist | Management | Non-combat | Base operations |

### Team Development
- **Early**: Mobile squads of 3-5.
- **Middle**: Bases of 20-50 with clear responsibilities.
- **Late**: Factions of hundreds with hierarchical management.

### Resource Cycle
```
Explore → Acquire resources → Build base → Grow stronger → Explore farther
```

## 8. Common Pitfalls

### Must Avoid
- ❌ A protagonist who rescues everyone without boundaries.
- ❌ Resources so easy to acquire that the apocalypse loses its atmosphere.
- ❌ Monster fighting without human dilemmas.
- ❌ Late-story invincibility that eliminates tension.

### Recommended Practices
- ✅ Give the protagonist principles without indiscriminate altruism.
- ✅ Keep resources tight and choices necessary.
- ✅ Make human conflict a central attraction.
- ✅ Always retain a stronger threat.
