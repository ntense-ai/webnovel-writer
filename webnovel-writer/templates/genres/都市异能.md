# Urban Superpower Fiction Template

> Runtime preset identifier: `都市异能` (preserved for taxonomy and initialization compatibility).

> **Core appeal**: Familiar modern life + the intrusion of extraordinary powers. Find the extraordinary within the ordinary.

---

## Recommended Creative Constraints (Optional)
- Recommended constraint packs: Pack M12 / Pack M05
- General overlay: Pack U03

## 1. Three Stages of Spiritual Awakening

### The Hidden Era
- **Status**: Superpowered people are rare and strictly controlled by the authorities. Ordinary people do not know powers exist.
- **Conflict**: The protagonist awakens, hides their identity, and evades official investigations and hostile pursuit.
- **Atmosphere**: Mystery, oppression, urban legends.
- **Hard rule**: Any major disturbance ordinary people might notice (explosion/collapse/fire/multiple casualties/road closure) must be followed by a passage explaining the real-world aftermath and concealment mechanism, in this chapter or the next.

### The Outbreak Era
- **Status**: Spiritual energy rises, plants and animals mutate, and awakenings multiply. The secret can no longer be kept.
- **Conflict**: Social order destabilizes and old and new factions reshuffle. Authorities reveal the existence of powers and establish academies.
- **Atmosphere**: Chaos, opportunity, passionate struggle.

### The New Order
- **Status**: Powers become normal; technology and cultivation combine. Humanity establishes fortified cities against supernatural beasts.
- **Conflict**: War between species (humans versus demons/otherworldly invaders).
- **Atmosphere**: Epic, apocalyptic, a struggle for survival.

---

## 2. Factional Competition

### The State
- **Features**: Guardians of order, with the greatest resources and the most rules.
- **Relationship to protagonist**: Recruitment? Cooperation? A wanted notice?
- **Agency names**: Dragon Group, Special Administration Bureau, Bureau 749, Night Watchers.

### The Underground
- **Conglomerates**: Seek to control powered people with money and research immortality.
- **Cults**: Worship otherworldly evil gods and manufacture chaos.
- **Guilds**: Mutual-aid/mercenary organizations formed by powered people themselves.

---

## 3. Power System Design

### Awakening Ranks
- **E rank**: Physical enhancement; slightly stronger than an ordinary person.
- **C rank**: Elemental/special powers capable of countering modern weapons.
- **A rank**: National-level strategic power; one person can fight an army division.
- **S rank**: Nuclear deterrent level; a walking nuclear bomb.

### Ability Categories
- **Elemental**: Fire, lightning, ice (high damage).
- **Enhancement**: Strength, speed, regeneration (high survivability).
- **Mental**: Telekinesis, hypnosis, precognition (uncanny).
- **Special**: Space, time, causality (reality-breaking).

---

## 4. Classic Payoff Patterns

### Hidden Strength Revealed
- **Scene**: A rich heir/thug humiliates the protagonist, assuming they are an ordinary student/worker.
- **Explosion**: The protagonist crushes them with one finger, displays an S-rank license, or reveals terrifying powers.

### Gemstone Gambling/Treasure Appraisal (Golden Eyes)
- **Scene**: In an antiques market, an expert misjudges an item and the protagonist finds a bargain.
- **Payoff**: Buy spiritually charged jade/artifacts for almost nothing.

### The Campus Beauty's Bodyguard
- **Scene**: Assigned to protect a wealthy young woman/campus beauty and live with her.
- **Payoff**: Romantic tension, defeating rival suitors, and ultimately gaining love and wealth.

---

## 5. Real-World Aftermath and Concealment (Hidden Era Only)

> **Purpose**: Make the world feel tangible and prevent readers asking, "Nobody cares about an explosion that big?"

### One Required Aftermath Detail
Choose 1-2 details below and dramatize them through specific wording, timing, or character reactions rather than a passing mention:
- **Scene management**: Police cordons, fire engines/ambulances, road closures, temporary power cuts/lockdowns.
- **Removing traces**: Reviewing surveillance footage, requiring phone videos to be deleted, taking witnesses away for statements.
- **Public narrative**: Fragmentary information in WeChat groups/short-video platforms plus a plausible official explanation (construction accident/gas explosion/high-voltage electrical fire).
- **Superpowered-world rules**: A special agency/night watchers intervene; show how civilian knowledge is handled.

### Writing Advice (Keep the Pace Moving)
- One brief ordinary-person viewpoint (driver/security guard/property manager/group chat) plus one sentence of official explanation is enough to establish the hidden era.
