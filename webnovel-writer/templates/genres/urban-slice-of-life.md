# Urban Slice-of-Life Template

> Runtime preset identifier: `urban-slice-of-life` (English taxonomy and initialization preset).

> **Core appeal**: Authentic everyday life + an ordinary person's rise + relatable details. Find the extraordinary in the ordinary and let readers recognize themselves.

---

## Recommended Creative Constraints (Optional)
- Recommended constraint pack: Pack M03
- General overlay: Pack U02

## 1. Core Subgenres

### Career Struggle
- **Features**: Workplace effort, promotions, pay rises, career success.
- **Core payoffs**: Superior performance, promotions that silence doubters, financial freedom.
- **Representative works**: *Go Lala Go!*, *The First Half of My Life*, *Ode to Joy*.

### Neighborhood Life
- **Features**: Ordinary people's everyday lives and lived-in atmosphere.
- **Core payoffs**: Practical wisdom, human warmth and coldness, small joys.
- **Representative works**: *Reply 1988*, *My Own Swordsman*.

### Entrepreneurial Rise
- **Features**: Start from nothing and achieve business success.
- **Core payoffs**: Business acumen, capital operations, accumulating wealth.
- **Representative works**: *Like a Flowing River*, *Feather Flies to the Sky*, *Under the Zhengyang Gate*.

### Urban Rebirth
- **Features**: Return to the past and change one's life.
- **Core payoffs**: Foresight, investment windfalls, repairing regrets.
- **Representative works**: *Rebirth of a God-Level Scholar*, *Rebirth of a Perfect Era*.

## 2. Worldbuilding: Urban Society

### Social Classes
| Class | Features | Resources |
|------|------|------|
| Wealthy | Capital owners | Unlimited |
| Middle class | White-collar elite | Ample |
| Salaried workers | Ordinary employees | Limited |
| Bottom tier | Laborers | Scarce |

### Workplace Ecosystem
| Role | Features | Relationship to protagonist |
|------|------|-----------|
| Owner | Decision-maker | Must win over |
| Supervisor | Direct manager | Potential enemy or ally |
| Colleague | Competitor | Cooperation and competition |
| Subordinate | Executor | Train and deploy |
| Client | Resource provider | Maintain relationship |

### Resource System
- **Money**: The most direct resource.
- **Connections**: Relationship networks.
- **Ability**: Professional skills.
- **Information**: Intelligence advantage.
- **Time**: The scarcest resource.

## 4. Classic Payoff Patterns

### Performance Dominance
- **Scene**: Work assignments/project competition.
- **Payoff**: The protagonist vastly outperforms rivals and wins new respect.
- **Key**: Show the process, not only the outcome.

### Promotion Reversal
- **Scene**: Someone previously looked down on the protagonist.
- **Payoff**: After promotion, that person must report to the protagonist.
- **Key**: Establish groundwork; do not make it overly contrived.

### Investment Windfall
- **Scene**: The protagonist invests in a project/stock.
- **Payoff**: Huge returns and financial freedom.
- **Key**: Give the information a plausible source.

### Connections Pay Off
- **Scene**: Relationships matter at a crucial moment.
- **Payoff**: Previously cultivated contacts provide major help.
- **Key**: Establish those contacts beforehand.

## 5. Outline Structure (Standard: 1.5 Million Chinese Characters)

### Volume One: Starting Out (Chapters 1-80)
- **Core**: Enter the workplace/start a business.
- **Status**: Bottom tier.
- **Climax**: First proof of ability wins recognition.

### Volume Two: A Foothold (Chapters 81-180)
- **Core**: Establish stability and foundations.
- **Status**: Frontline.
- **Climax**: First promotion/successful project.

### Volume Three: Rise (Chapters 181-300)
- **Core**: Advance rapidly and stand out.
- **Status**: Middle ranks.
- **Climax**: A major project succeeds; enter the inner circle.

### Volume Four: Pinnacle (Chapters 301-450)
- **Core**: Become an industry elite.
- **Status**: Senior ranks.
- **Climax**: Reach the career summit.

### Volume Five: Fulfillment (Chapters 451-550)
- **Core**: Achieve a fulfilling life.
- **Status**: Top tier.
- **Climax**: Success in both career and family.

| Benefactor | Guidance and help | Mentor/talent spotter |
| Rival | Creates conflict | Competition |
| Ally | Fights alongside | Partner |
| Schemer | Creates obstacles | Must guard against |

## 7. Conflict Design

### Workplace Conflicts
| Conflict | Source | Resolution |
|------|------|------|
| Performance competition | Colleagues | Demonstrate ability |
| Power struggle | Superiors | Choose sides/protect oneself |
| Resource competition | Departments | Negotiate/cooperate |
| Layoff crisis | Company | Prove value |

### Life Conflicts
| Conflict | Source | Resolution |
|------|------|------|
| Financial pressure | Mortgage/family support | Increase income and reduce spending |
| Family tensions | Relatives | Communication and understanding |
| Relationship trouble | Partner | Maintain the relationship |
| Health issues | Self | Adjust lifestyle |

---

## 8. Common Pitfalls

### Must Avoid
- ❌ Too much routine without conflict.
- ❌ Too few payoffs.
- ❌ A passive protagonist.
- ❌ Overly obvious special advantages.

### Recommended Practices
- ✅ Find conflict within everyday life.
- ✅ Maintain payoff pacing.
- ✅ Let the protagonist take initiative.
- ✅ Establish groundwork for success.
