# Western Fantasy Template

> Runtime preset identifier: `西幻` (preserved for taxonomy and initialization compatibility).

> **Core appeal**: Divine and demonic systems + knightly adventures + contractual costs. Tell a mortal epic in the space between gods and demons.

## 1. Core Subgenres

### Knightly Adventure
- **Features**: Classic D&D-style adventures; parties fight monsters and level up.
- **Core payoffs**: Teamwork, collecting equipment, boss battles.
- **Representative works**: *Necromancer*, *The Wizard World*, *A Wizard's Secret*.

### Magic Academy
- **Features**: Study at a magical school; campus life plus adventure.
- **Core payoffs**: Awakening talent, academy competition, a mentor's legacy.
- **Representative works**: *The Wizard World*, *Throne of Magical Arcana*, *Scholar's Advanced Technological System*.

### Theocratic Church
- **Features**: Gods are real and the church controls the world.
- **Core payoffs**: Religious maneuvering, challenging divine authority, revealing truth.
- **Representative works**: *Lord of Mysteries*, *The Sacred Ruins*, *Supernatural Revival*.

### Territory Management
- **Features**: Gain land, develop a faction, and compete for continental supremacy.
- **Core payoffs**: Building territory, recruiting talent, commanding wars.
- **Representative works**: *Age of Lords*, *Starting from Zero*, *Glory of the Lord*.

### Dark Epic
- **Features**: A brutal world, gray morality, and resistance to fate.
- **Core payoffs**: Desperate survival, moral choices, epic battles.
- **Representative works**: *A Song of Ice and Fire*, *The Witcher*, *Dark Souls*.

## 2. Worldbuilding: Fantasy Society

### Species System
| Species | Features | Role |
|------|------|------|
| Humans | Adaptable, reproduce quickly | Dominant species |
| Elves | Long-lived, magically attuned | Noble but arrogant |
| Dwarves | Gifted craftspeople, resilient | Master smiths |
| Orcs | Strong, tribal | Barbarians/enemies |
| Dragons | Top-tier power, rare | Bosses/allies |
| Demons | Contractual dealings, corruption | Tempters |

### Class Structure
```
Gods/divine messengers
    ↓
Pope/archbishops
    ↓
Kings/grand dukes
    ↓
Nobles/knights
    ↓
Merchants/craftspeople
    ↓
Peasants/slaves
```

### Faction Landscape
- **Church**: Represents divine authority; controls faith and some secular power.
- **Kingdoms**: Secular rulers who both cooperate and clash with the church.
- **Mage towers**: Relatively independent magical research institutions.
- **Adventurers' guilds**: Mercenary organizations and quest brokers.
- **Dark factions**: Cults/demons/undead; the main enemies.

## 3. Magic System Design

### Standard Magic Levels
| Level | Title | Abilities | Social status |
|------|------|------|----------|
| First circle | Apprentice | Basic spells | Ordinary person |
| Third circle | Mage | Combat spells | Respected |
| Fifth circle | High mage | Domain spells | Treated like nobility |
| Seventh circle | Archmage | Forbidden spells | National adviser |
| Ninth circle | Legendary mage | Alters rules | Power of a nation |

### Schools of Magic
| School | Features | Cost |
|------|------|------|
| Elemental | Fire/ice/lightning/wind | Mental energy |
| Necromancy | Controls undead | Vitality/sanity |
| Summoning | Summons creatures | Contractual costs |
| Enchantment | Enhances objects | Materials |
| Divine magic | Granted by gods | Devotion |
| Blood magic | Blood as a medium | Life/blood |

### Magical Costs (Important)
- **Mental energy**: Spent casting; recoverable.
- **Vitality**: Cost of powerful spells; difficult to recover.
- **Sanity**: Forbidden knowledge can cause madness.
- **Contractual bonds**: Deals with higher beings carry a price.

## 4. Classic Payoff Patterns

### Bloodline Awakening
- **Scene**: The protagonist discovers a special bloodline (dragon/demon/divine descendant).
- **Payoff**: Overwhelming talent and unique inherited abilities.
- **Key**: Bloodlines require costs or limitations.

### Acquiring a Legacy
- **Scene**: Discover ruins belonging to an ancient archmage/knight.
- **Payoff**: Gain a powerful legacy and leap forward in strength.
- **Key**: Unlock it gradually rather than reaching the summit instantly.

### Contract Summoning
- **Scene**: Sign a contract with a powerful being.
- **Payoff**: Gain a powerful helper/ability.
- **Key**: Contracts must cost something; no free benefits.

### Identity Reversal
- **Scene**: A supposedly insignificant person has an astonishing identity.
- **Payoff**: The revelation silences everyone who looked down on them.
- **Example**: A wandering knight is the prince of a lost kingdom.

## 5. Outline Structure (Standard: 2 Million Chinese Characters)

### Volume One: Origins (Chapters 1-80)
- **Core**: Awakening/enrollment; build foundational abilities.
- **Setting**: Academy/small town.
- **Climax**: First display of talent defeats a powerful peer.

### Volume Two: Trials (Chapters 81-180)
- **Core**: Venture out and form a party.
- **Setting**: Within the kingdom.
- **Climax**: Complete a major quest and gain recognition.

### Volume Three: Rise (Chapters 181-350)
- **Core**: Become entangled in major factions' struggles.
- **Setting**: Multiple countries.
- **Climax**: Become central to a major faction.

### Volume Four: Supremacy (Chapters 351-550)
- **Core**: Participate in continental conflicts.
- **Setting**: Continent-wide.
- **Climax**: Defeat the main enemy and expose the truth.

### Volume Five: War of Gods (Chapters 551-700)
- **Core**: Confront gods/supreme beings.
- **Setting**: Multiple planes.
- **Climax**: Become a god/change the world's rules.

| Character | Class | Function | Personality |
|------|------|------|------|
| Protagonist | Mage/knight | Main damage dealer | Growth-oriented |
| Warrior | Knight/fighter | Frontline tank | Loyal and dependable |
| Priest | Cleric | Healing/support | Gentle/serious |
| Rogue | Assassin/ranger | Scouting/traps | Clever and cunning |
| Mage | Elementalist/summoner | Backline damage | Scholarly |

### Party Development
- **Early**: Temporary team, adjusting to one another.
- **Middle**: Stable party with coordinated teamwork.
- **Late**: Individual growth, separations, and reunions.

| Deity | Domain | Followers | Attitude |
|------|------|------|------|
| God of light | Order/justice | Church | Order above all |
| God of war | Battle/glory | Knights | Honors strength |
| God of knowledge | Magic/wisdom | Mages | Neutral |
| God of nature | Life/forests | Elves/druids | Protects nature |
| God of death | Undead/reincarnation | Necromancers | Impartial |
| God of chaos | Destruction/change | Cults | Hostile |

### Acquiring Divine Magic
- **Devout prayer**: Deeper faith produces stronger magic.
- **Divine contract**: Make a contract with a god.
- **Chosen status**: A god selects someone and grants divine power.

## 8. Common Pitfalls

### Must Avoid
- ❌ Overloading readers with incomprehensible worldbuilding terminology.
- ❌ Copying species conventions without originality.
- ❌ Cost-free magic and an invincible protagonist.
- ❌ Supporting characters without independent personalities.

### Recommended Practices
- ✅ Reveal the world gradually as the journey proceeds.
- ✅ Give species distinctive features.
- ✅ Define clear magical costs and limits.
- ✅ Give supporting characters their own storylines.
